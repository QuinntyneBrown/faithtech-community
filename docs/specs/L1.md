# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Sources: the proposals in `docs/proposals/` (a proposal wins on conflict) and `docs/mocks/`. The mock area tag follows each title. The product is a multi-tenant platform: every requirement below applies within one organisation unless it says otherwise, and FaithTech is the first organisation (`L1-025`).

## L1-001: Identity and accounts (AUTH)
The system shall let anyone create an account with a locally stored, securely hashed password, verify their email, sign in through OAuth2 authorization code + PKCE, sign out, and reset a forgotten password. Google is an external sign-in method: a person may sign in or create an account with a Google account whose email Google has verified. An account is global; signing up on an organisation's host also creates the person's profile in that organisation.

## L1-002: Profiles and photos (PROF)
Every member shall have a profile in each organisation they belong to, with a name, an optional home chapter and up to ten photos, one of them featured, visible only to signed-in members of that organisation.

## L1-003: Roles and role assignment (ROLE)
The system shall let a profile hold several of its organisation's roles, each optionally tagged with a chapter, and shall restrict who is permitted to grant or remove each role to the holders of the roles the organisation's grant rules allow, and to administrators.

## L1-004: Invites (INV)
Members permitted to invite shall be able to invite people by email into their organisation, optionally pre-assigning a role they may grant, with invites that expire. Members permitted to connect shall also have a reusable personal QR code that lets someone they meet join the organisation and connect with them.

## L1-005: Private contacts (CON)
Members permitted to keep contacts shall be able to create, search, annotate, photograph, archive, restore and permanently delete contacts that are private to them, see them as a grid of person cards, and link a contact to the person's real profile when they sign up, automatically when the person joins through the member's QR code. A contact's photos are as private as the contact.

## L1-006: Partner pipeline (PART)
Members permitted to manage partners shall share one organisation-wide partner pipeline, shown as a Kanban board with one column per stage the organisation defines.

## L1-007: Team (TEAM)
The system shall show the team: everyone who holds a role the organisation shows on its team list, filterable by role and by chapter.

## L1-008: Events (EVT)
Members permitted to manage events shall be able to create, edit and cancel events, members-only or public, each of a type the organisation defines and optionally belonging to a chapter. Members and guests shall be able to RSVP, and every event shall offer an .ics download.

## L1-009: Global search (SRCH)
Signed-in members shall be able to search their organisation's profiles, events, partners, resources, notes, their own private contacts and the Agile work they can access, with results filtered by permission.

## L1-010: Notifications (NOTIF)
The system shall notify users in-app and by email about events that concern them.

## L1-011: Announcements (ANN)
Members permitted to send announcements shall be able to send them to everyone in the organisation, to the holders of a role, or to an event's RSVP list. Recipients shall be able to opt out of announcement emails.

## L1-012: Agile team workspaces (AGILE)
Members permitted to own workspaces shall be able to run their team's work in workspaces using Scrum or Kanban, with Initiatives, Epics, Stories and Tasks, a board, a backlog and sprints.

## L1-013: Planning Poker (AGILE)
Workspace members shall be able to estimate stories together in live Planning Poker sessions.

## L1-014: Organisation administration and audit (ADMIN)
An organisation's administrators shall have full override within their organisation, including suspending members and assigning any role. Every override and every role change shall be recorded in the organisation's audit log.

## L1-015: Security
The system shall enforce every authorization rule on the server, validate all input, and protect against the OWASP Top 10.

## L1-016: Accessibility and responsive design
Every screen shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA and work at viewport widths from extra small (<640px) to extra large (>=1280px), in light and dark themes.

## L1-017: Performance and reliability
The system shall respond quickly under expected load, degrade gracefully, and be observable in production.

## L1-018: Error and system pages (SYS)
The system shall show clear, recoverable pages for not found, forbidden, server error, offline, unknown host and not-a-member conditions.

## L1-019: Home (HOME)
Every signed-in member shall land on a home page that summarizes what is coming up and what has happened, tailored to what their roles let them do.

## L1-020: Resources (RES)
Members permitted to add resources shall be able to add decks, logos, images, training materials and templates as uploaded files or https links, organised by the types the organisation defines and by tags, each private to its owner, shared with chosen members and roles, or visible to every signed-in member. Members shall be able to find, open and download the resources visible to them, and global search shall include them.

## L1-021: Marketing page (MKT)
An anonymous visitor at an organisation's public host shall see a public marketing page that explains that organisation's community with a short brochure video and lets them sign in, sign up or sign in with Google without leaving the page, then lands them in the community app on the organisation's host with a real session.

## L1-022: Docs & notes (NOTES)
Signed-in members shall be able to write block-based pages, such as meeting notes, playbooks and how-tos, and format, link and nest them. Each page shall be private to its owner, shared with chosen members and roles, or visible to every signed-in member, and only its owner or an administrator shall be able to change it. Members shall be able to find and read the pages visible to them, see which pages link to a page, and find pages through global search.

## L1-023: Private preview and deployment (WALL)
While the platform is in private preview, the system shall be deployed over HTTPS with every organisation's community host (app, API and identity server) and public host (marketing page) behind one server-enforced wall: until a visitor unlocks it with the owner's passphrase, every request to any host shall be refused and the only page shown shall be a neutral unlock page that reveals nothing about the product. After unlocking, every host shall behave exactly as it does without the wall. The owner shall be able to rotate the passphrase, revoke every unlock and lift the wall by configuration.

## L1-024: Reports (RPT)
Members permitted to view reports shall see a read-only report of their organisation's numbers (members, events held, RSVPs and check-ins, partners confirmed and announcements sent), filtered by chapter and period, with charts and tables they can export. Private contacts shall never be counted.

## L1-025: Organisations and tenancy (ORG)
One deployment shall host many organisations. Every record shall belong to exactly one organisation, resolved from the request host, and nothing shall be visible across organisations. An account shall be global and may hold one profile in each organisation it belongs to; a member shall be able to switch between their organisations. FaithTech shall be the first organisation, provisioned by seed so that everything it does today is unchanged.

## L1-026: Role definitions and capabilities (ROLE)
The platform shall define a fixed catalogue of capabilities, and the server shall authorise every action by capability. An organisation shall define its own roles as named bundles of capabilities, with flags for the team list, chapter scoping and oversight, and shall define grant rules saying which roles may grant which. Changes to a role shall take effect on the holders' next request, and an organisation shall always keep at least one administrator.

## L1-027: Chapters (CHAP)
An organisation may define chapters: a single level of subdivisions with a label of its choosing, such as City, Campus or Region. Profiles, events and role assignments may be tagged with a chapter, and lists may be filtered by chapter. Chapters shall never limit what a member can see; they may limit which role assignments a chapter-tagged grantor may change.

## L1-028: Organisation settings and taxonomies (ORGSET)
Members permitted to manage organisation settings shall be able to set the organisation's name, short name and chapter label and to define its chapters, partner stages, event types and resource types as ordered lists whose entries are archived, never deleted, once in use. Every change shall be audited.

## L1-029: Platform operations (PLAT)
Platform operators, who belong to no organisation, shall be able to provision an organisation with its host and first administrator, suspend and lift it, and read a platform audit log. Operators shall never read an organisation's data.
