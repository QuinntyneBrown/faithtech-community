# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Sources: `idea.txt`, `decisions.txt` (wins on conflict) and `docs/mocks/`. The mock area tag follows each title.

## L1-001: Identity and accounts (AUTH)
The system shall let anyone create an account with a locally stored, securely hashed password, verify their email, sign in through OAuth2 authorization code + PKCE, sign out, and reset a forgotten password. Google is an external sign-in method: a person may sign in or create an account with a Google account whose email Google has verified.

## L1-002: Profiles and photos (PROF)
Every account shall have a profile with a name and up to ten photos, one of them featured, visible only to signed-in members.

## L1-003: Roles and role assignment (ROLE)
The system shall support multiple global roles per profile and shall restrict who is permitted to grant or remove each role.

## L1-004: Invites (INV)
Leads and System Admins shall be able to invite people by email, optionally pre-assigning a role, with invites that expire. City Leads and System Admins shall also have a reusable personal QR code that lets someone they meet join and connect with them.

## L1-005: Private contacts (CON)
Leads shall be able to create, search, annotate, photograph, archive, restore and permanently delete contacts that are private to them, see them as a grid of person cards, and link a contact to the person's real profile when they sign up, automatically when the person joins through the lead's QR code. A contact's photos are as private as the contact.

## L1-006: Partner pipeline (PART)
Leads shall share one partner pipeline, shown as a Kanban board with the stages Lead, In Funnel and Confirmed.

## L1-007: City team (TEAM)
The system shall show the team: everyone who holds a lead or volunteer role.

## L1-008: Events (EVT)
Leads shall be able to create, edit and cancel events, members-only or public. Members and guests shall be able to RSVP, and every event shall offer an .ics download. Hackathon is an event type.

## L1-009: Global search (SRCH)
Signed-in members shall be able to search profiles, events, partners, their own private contacts and the Agile work they can access, with results filtered by permission.

## L1-010: Notifications (NOTIF)
The system shall notify users in-app and by email about events that concern them.

## L1-011: Announcements (ANN)
Communications Leads shall be able to send announcements to everyone, to the holders of a role, or to an event's RSVP list. Recipients shall be able to opt out of announcement emails.

## L1-012: Agile team workspaces (AGILE)
City Leads shall be able to run their team's work in workspaces using Scrum or Kanban, with Initiatives, Epics, Stories and Tasks, a board, a backlog and sprints.

## L1-013: Planning Poker (AGILE)
Workspace members shall be able to estimate stories together in live Planning Poker sessions.

## L1-014: System administration and audit (ADMIN)
System Admins shall have full override, including suspending users and assigning any role. Every admin action shall be recorded in an audit log.

## L1-015: Security
The system shall enforce every authorization rule on the server, validate all input, and protect against the OWASP Top 10.

## L1-016: Accessibility and responsive design
Every screen shall meet WCAG 2.2 AA and work at viewport widths from extra small (<640px) to extra large (>=1280px), in light and dark themes.

## L1-017: Performance and reliability
The system shall respond quickly under expected load, degrade gracefully, and be observable in production.

## L1-018: Error and system pages (SYS)
The system shall show clear, recoverable pages for not found, forbidden, server error and offline conditions.

## L1-019: Home (HOME)
Every signed-in member shall land on a home page that summarizes what is coming up and what has happened, tailored to their roles.

## L1-020: Resources (RES)
Leads, Staff and System Admins shall be able to add decks, logos, images, training materials and templates as uploaded files or https links, organised by type and tags, each private to its owner, shared with chosen members and roles, or visible to every signed-in member. Members shall be able to find, open and download the resources visible to them, and global search shall include them.

## L1-021: Marketing page (MKT)
An anonymous visitor at `www.faithtech.community` shall see a public marketing page that explains FaithTech Community with a short brochure video and lets them sign in, sign up or sign in with Google without leaving the page, then lands them in the community app with a real session.

## L1-022: Docs & notes (NOTES)
Signed-in members shall be able to write block-based pages, such as meeting notes, playbooks and how-tos, and format, link and nest them. Each page shall be private to its owner, shared with chosen members and roles, or visible to every signed-in member, and only its owner or a System Admin shall be able to change it. Members shall be able to find and read the pages visible to them, see which pages link to a page, and find pages through global search.

## L1-023: Private preview and deployment (WALL)
While the product is in private preview, the system shall be deployed to `app.faithtech.community` (community app, API and identity server) and `www.faithtech.community` (marketing page) over HTTPS behind a server-enforced wall: until a visitor unlocks it with the owner's passphrase, every request to either host shall be refused and the only page shown shall be a neutral unlock page that reveals nothing about the product. After unlocking, both hosts shall behave exactly as they do without the wall. The owner shall be able to rotate the passphrase, revoke every unlock and lift the wall by configuration.
