# FaithTech SoCal × Baptize The World Hackathon (24 October 2026)

The participant hub for the FaithTech SoCal × Baptize The World Hackathon, held on Saturday 24 October 2026 at The Robertson Triangle, 3390 Robertson Pl, Los Angeles. Saved here as published so the agenda, tracks, judging criteria, slides and parking maps stay with the project after the event page goes away.

Source: the public page at <https://claude.ai/artifact/QyEXkYrheiwpsJkuustM95> (version 1792894398-78b8, "Updated October 22, 2026"), copied without changes. `index.html` SHA-256 `9846ed90d61dd1552ae126835f3bd97c26ab1f097d98981f69bb4a1eb78acda0`.

## How to open

Open `index.html` from disk in Chrome. It is self-contained apart from the Inter web font (Google Fonts) and the outbound links (challenge repo, Pathfinder, Typeform submission and feedback forms, donations, YouTube, Maps, Luma, Eventbrite). The slide viewer and parking map lightbox work offline. On the day the agenda highlighted the current session from the visitor's clock in Pacific time.

## The day in brief

- **Schedule:** doors 9:00 AM, worship 9:15, welcome 9:30, Baptize The World and the tracks 9:45, honors and judging 10:00, teams form 10:05, Pathfinder 10:25, Lament 10:30, Discern 11:00, lunch 12:00, build 12:30 to 4:00 with a prayer pause at 2:00, Steward and demo prep 3:00 (dry run 3:45), code freeze 4:00, demos 4:15, group photo and feedback 5:00, honors 5:15, commissioning 5:30, doors close 6:00.
- **Tracks:** 1 Mobile-first church onboarding (primary focus), 2 Testimony sharing, 3 Post-baptism discipleship. Briefs and test data: <https://github.com/FaithTechGlobalLabs/faithtech-socal-hackathon>.
- **Process:** teams of 3 to 5 with a Project Lead and a Dev work the Create Cycle (Lament, Discern, Build, Steward) in Pathfinder.
- **Build rules:** start fresh, any stack, test data only, credit sources, code freeze at 4:00, public repo.
- **Demos:** 3 minutes (track 0:30, Tend 1:30, Examine 0:30, Testify 0:30) plus 2 minutes of questions.
- **Judging:** 1 to 5 on redemptive potential (40%), usable by the end user (30%) and technical viability (30%). Honors: Most Redemptive (top score), Most Collaborative (mentors' pick), Most Creative (judges' pick).
- **Mentors and judges:** Travis Johnson, Dana Calit, Holly Dunlap, Arren Bustamante (mentor), Pastor Calin (Baptize The World), Jesse Eshleman (FaithTech, judge).
