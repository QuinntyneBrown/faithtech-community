Toronto city team playbook

How we run FaithTech Toronto: who does what, when, and where everything lives. Start here if you’re new to the city team, and ask in the team channel if something looks out of date. We now meet on Mondays Tuesdays. The room code is FT-TOR-26, and our public page is faithtech.community (opens in a new tab).
Our rhythm
Most months follow the same pattern. Everything for the night itself is in 🛠️Builders Night run sheet.

  • Builders Night on the first Thursday
  • City Team Prayer & Planning on the last Tuesday
  • A coffee with each partner in the funnel
