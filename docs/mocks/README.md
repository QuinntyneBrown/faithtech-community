# FaithTech Community mocks

Static HTML mocks for every page, dialog and notification, built from [`idea.txt`](../../idea.txt) and [`decisions.txt`](../../decisions.txt), styled with the [FaithTech Community design system](../design-system/README.md) (tokens and components inherited from Shelfie).

## How to open

- Open [`index.html`](index.html) from disk in Chrome. It is the gallery of every screen and state.
- `?theme=dark` / `?theme=light` forces a theme; `t` toggles it. `?chrome=0` hides the mock bar and notes.
- Each mock's bottom bar links its sibling states.
- A button that opens another mock (Remove photo opens Delete photo, say) stays a `<button type="button">` and names its target in `data-href`; `mock.js` follows it. Never turn a button into a link to make it navigate.

## Open questions and assumptions

1. Agile work management follows the **AGILE WORK MANAGEMENT** section of `decisions.txt`. There are no open questions left; see the mapping below.
2. Guest RSVP lists show the guest's email to leads only.
3. Deleting a contact permanently requires typing DELETE.
4. The UI is in English only, and dates use the "Tue 21 Oct" format.
5. The brand uses Shelfie's violet accent and rounded type, unchanged, as requested. Replace the tokens if FaithTech brand colours are supplied.
6. Resources mocks follow [`docs/proposals/resources.md`](../proposals/resources.md), drawn before the `L1-020` requirements (now `L2-055` to `L2-066`); see the mapping below.
7. Adding Resources made the top bar too wide at 1024–1279px (it already overflowed slightly at 1024px). The secondary links (Team, Announcements, Resources, People, Admin) now join the bar from 1536px (`--layout-breakpoint-2xl`) and sit behind More below that. The Angular `TopBarComponent` must follow when Resources is built.
8. Resources has no separate share dialog: sharing is the Shared choice of “Who can see it” in `dialogs/add-resource/shared` and the edit dialog, and “Change who can see it” on the resource page opens `dialogs/edit-resource` on that picker (`L2-061` AC5). The `dialogs/share-resource` folder named in the feature prompt is therefore not drawn, and Edit resource carries `L2-061`.
9. Pre-existing: Grace's top bar is about 18px too wide at exactly 768px (Home, Events, Contacts, Partners, Workspaces, More). Not changed here.
10. Settled in `L2-061`: a share with a role follows role changes live, and only the members holding the role at share time are notified. The shared mocks say "14 people today", which matches.
11. The Dynamics 365 adoption mocks follow the "Yes" rows of [`docs/proposals/sharepoint-dynamics-features.md`](../proposals/sharepoint-dynamics-features.md) (activity timeline, dashboards and reports, duplicate detection) plus event check-in from the "Partly" event-management row, drawn before any requirement exists; see the mapping below. Assumed: the activity timeline is per partner and shared by every lead (contacts keep their private notes); reports are read-only for Staff and System Admin and never count private contacts; a walk-in with a member's email links to that profile; duplicates are detected on the normalised website host or the normalised name (never the email domain: shared mail providers would flag unrelated partners), and "Not the same" hides the pair for everyone. The decisions brief is now binding; the mocks were brought in line with it on 2026-10-22: no recap email after check-in (deferred), Reports in the shell only for Staff and System Admin, and the merge shown as of "today" (Tue 21 Oct) in the cast's calendar.
12. **My QR code** lives on the invites page, beside "Invite someone", and only City Leads and System Admins see it (`dialogs/my-qr-code`). The QR code is drawn dark on a light plate in both themes so any camera reads it.
13. The connect page is routed at `/meet/:token`, not `/connect/:token`, because `/connect/*` already belongs to the identity server (OpenIddict) behind the same origin (`pages/connect`). Tomás Ortega is the person who scans Grace's code.

The Notes mocks' top bar and drawer gain a **Notes** item after Resources (secondary, so it sits behind More below 1536px); the other mocks' navigation is not redrawn; the built `TopBarComponent` shows it to every signed-in member. The Notes mocks follow the shipped `notes` library and the `ft-block-editor`, `ft-slash-menu`, `ft-format-toolbar`, `ft-page-links`, `ft-save-status` and `ft-emoji-picker` components (copy, labels and states); the editor's text boxes, handles, menus and live region are drawn with the mock classes in `design-system/assets/components.css`.
15. The notes brief named Esther Nakamura as the Staff author of "Q4 planning notes"; in this cast Esther is an Austin City Lead and Daniel Reyes is Staff, so the page belongs to Daniel.

20. Every new settings dialog has a `success` state: the dialog has closed and the page shows the toast. A failed save keeps the input and offers **Try again** in place of the primary button (`L2-147` AC5).
21. Stages & types has one Active / Archived filter for its three lists rather than one per list. Set as initial and Counts as (Open or Confirmed) are row-menu actions with a toast and no dialog, as the taxonomies design proposed. The open row menu is drawn inline (`.mock-overflow-visible`, mock only); the product renders it in a CDK overlay.
22. The Archive role dialog shows the API's 409 refusals (holders, still in use) inside the dialog with only Close. 23. People gains the City filter (`L2-142` AC1).
24. A non-administrator who holds Manage roles or Manage organisation settings reaches the audit log from General (`/organisation/audit`); the page is headed "Audit log", has no Admin tabs and offers only the categories they may read (`pages/audit-log/partial`).
25. Name is up to 100 characters and short name up to 30, as the tenancy design and the provisioning validator say (`edit-general-settings`), resolving the organisation-settings design's open question.
26. The unknown-host and suspended pages follow the private-preview wall: neutral accent, no logo, product or organisation name.
