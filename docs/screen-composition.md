# Screen composition

Which `@faithtech-community/components` parts build each screen, and what is built locally in the feature library. Sources: the mocks in `docs/mocks/` (states listed per screen) and the public API of `frontend/projects/components/src/public-api.ts`. Mock-only chrome (`mock-bar`, `mock-note`) is excluded.

**Local UI** is markup a feature library writes itself. It is limited to page composition: headings that belong to the page, sections and the glue between `api` services and components. Anything reusable goes into `components` first (`AGENTS.md`).

**Add to components** marks a part the library does not have yet. It is built in `frontend/projects/components/` to match the named design-system page before the screen that needs it, never as local UI. No gaps are open: the Dynamics parts are all in the library now ([Summary](#summary-of-local-ui-to-build), item 14).

Coverage: the profiles screens (`profile`, `edit-profile`, `delete-photo`), the admin screens (`admin-users`, `audit-log`, `add-role`, `remove-role`, `suspend-user`), the invites screens (`invites`, `accept-invite`, `send-invite`, `my-qr-code`, `connect`), the team list (`team`), the events screens (`events`, `event-form`, `event`, `event-check-in`, `check-in-walk-in`, `cancel-rsvp`, `cancel-event`, `public-event`, `guest-cancel-rsvp`), settings (`settings`: Appearance and Email me when), the notifications screens (the bell and panel, `notifications`), the announcements screens (`announcements`, `compose-announcement`, `unsubscribe`), global search (`search`), the contacts screens (`contacts`, `contact`, `contact-form`, `archived-contacts`, `add-note`, `archive-contact`, `delete-contact`, `link-contact`, `add-contact-photo`, `edit-photo-description`, `delete-contact-photo`), the partners screens (`partners`, `partner`, `partner-form`, `add-partner`, `move-partner`, `log-activity`, `merge-partners`, `restore-partner`), Reports (`reports`) and the agile screens (`workspaces`, `team-board`, `backlog`, `work-item`, `workspace-settings`, `planning-poker` and their dialogs), the community app's `sign-in` and `sign-up` pages, the marketing app's page (`marketing`), the server-rendered private-preview wall (`private-preview`) and the resources screens (`resources`, `resource`, `archived-resources`, `add-resource`, `edit-resource`, `archive-resource`, `transfer-resource-owner`, `resource-toast`, the `resource-shared` bell and email, and the `resources` state of `search`) and the notes screens (`notes`, `note`, `archived-notes`, `share-note`, `note-settings`, `move-note`, `archive-note`, `restore-note`, `add-image`, `add-link`, `add-link-card`, `edit-alt-text`, `change-icon`, `note-toast`, the `note-shared` bell and email, and the `notes` state of `search`). Other screens are added here as their features are built.

## Pages

### Marketing page — `www.faithtech.community/` in the marketing app (`pages/marketing`: default, sign-in-busy, sign-in-invalid, sign-in-error, sign-in-unreachable, locked, suspended, unverified, sign-up, sign-up-invalid, sign-up-busy, sign-up-error, sign-up-success, google-busy, google-failed, video-unavailable, reduced-motion)

- Library: `SkipLinkComponent`, `TopBarComponent` `anonymous` with *Join now* (`ButtonComponent` `ghost`) and *Sign in* in `ftTopBarActions`, `ContainerComponent`, `LandingHeroComponent` (eyebrow, `<h1>` heading and lead; `ftHeroPanel` and `ftHeroMedia` slots), `CardComponent` holding `SignInFormComponent` or `SignUpFormComponent` (the same forms as the community app's pages, `divider="or"`) with `ButtonComponent` `variant="google"` *Sign in with Google* / *Sign up with Google* in `ftAuthAlternatives`, `ButtonComponent` `ghost` *Join now* / *Sign in* to switch the panel, `AlertComponent` (the unverified hand-off), `EmptyStateComponent` with `LinkComponent` (check your inbox, send it again), `VideoPlayerComponent` (poster, captions, transcript, unavailable), `GridComponent` `auto` of three `BenefitComponent`s (Why join), `CtaBandComponent` with *Join now*, `SiteFooterComponent` (shell).

The in-page forms are the page's purpose, so they are not a dialog (AGENTS.md "No inline forms" exception, recorded in the mock and design).

### Private preview wall — any path on `app.` and `www.faithtech.community` while locked, rendered by the server (`pages/private-preview`: default, busy, invalid, locked-out, error, redirect)

- Not an Angular screen: the API's private-preview gate renders it before either app bundle may load, so it cannot use `@faithtech-community/components` (ADR 0026). It composes the design system's classes instead: auth layout, `card`, `field` with `field__error`, `alert` (`warning` for locked-out, `danger` for error) and `btn btn--primary btn--lg btn--block`, under the neutral accent scope (`data-accent="neutral"`, design system `patterns/private-preview-wall.html`).
- Local (server template `backend/src/FaithTech.Community.Api/Middleware/PrivatePreview/wall.html`): the inline, nonce-protected copy of those rules read by role, the plain-HTML form (`csrf`, `returnUrl`, `passphrase`) and the small script that shows the busy state. No logo, product name, description, favicon or brand colour.

## Notifications

None for profiles. Admin and invites use `ToastService` success toasts on the invites page; role changes and accepted invites raise in-app notifications, shown by the bell and the notifications page. Announcements raise one in-app notification per recipient and, unless they opted out, an email whose HTML part follows the design system's `.email` pattern (`notifications/email/announcement`), with an unsubscribe link. Agile screens use `ToastService` toasts for saves, moves, archive and restore; member additions, assignments, Planning Poker invites and an owner losing City Lead raise in-app notifications and emails. Contacts and partners use `ToastService` toasts (archived, restored, deleted, partner added, a failed drag); a contact who verifies their email raises a `ContactSignedUp` in-app notification and an email to the owning lead; a person who joins or connects through a lead's QR code raises the same kind worded "{name} connected with you" (`notifications/bell/connected`, `notifications/email/connected`).

Partners, check-in and Reports raise no notifications. They use `ToastService`: "{name} is checked in" with *Undo* and the danger "Couldn’t save {name}’s check-in. Try again." with *Try again* (`pages/event-check-in`), the walk-in toasts (`event-check-in/walk-in-added`), "Marked {other} as a different partner" (`pages/partner/not-the-same`), "Merged {other} into {kept}" (`pages/partner/merged`), "{name} restored" (`pages/partner/restored`), "{Type} logged for {name}", and the danger "Couldn’t export the report. Try again." (`pages/reports/export-failed`). No new component.

Resources (`notifications/resource-toast`: success, info, warning, danger, batch-added, restored) use `ToastService` with `ToastComponent` variants: success "{title} added" ("Every signed-in member can find it now.") or "{n} resources added" with *View*; info "{title} archived" with *Undo*; warning "{n−m} of {n} resources added" with *Try again*; success "{title} restored" with *View*; danger "{title} wasn’t restored" with *Try again*. Sharing raises the `ResourceShared` notice "{Owner} shared {title} with you" in the bell and on the notifications page through `NoticeListComponent` (`notifications/bell/resource-shared`), and an email in the `.email` pattern with the heading "A resource was shared with you" and the button *Open the resource* (`notifications/email/resource-shared`); ownership transfer raises `ResourceOwnershipTransferred` the same way. No new component.

Notes (`notifications/note-toast`: archived, restored, danger, shared, moved) use `ToastService` with `ToastComponent` variants: info "{title} archived" ("Restore it from Archived.") with *Undo*, which restores the page and reopens it; success "{title} restored" ("It’s back in Notes, with the same visibility as before.") with *View*; danger "{title} wasn’t restored" ("We couldn’t reach FaithTech. It’s still archived.") when *Undo* fails; success "Sharing updated for {title}" ("Only you can see it now." / "The people you chose can read it now." / "Every signed-in member can find it now."); success "{title} moved" ("It’s now inside {parent}." / "It’s now at the top level."). Sharing raises the `NotePageShared` notice "{Owner} shared {title} with you" in the bell and on the notifications page through `NoticeListComponent`, and an email in the `.email` pattern with the heading "A page was shared with you" and the button *Open the page* (`notifications/email/note-shared`; it can't be turned off). Global search gains a "Notes ({n})" group in `SearchResultsComponent` (`pages/search/notes`: `IconTileComponent` `name="notes"`, title "{icon} {title}", meta "{you | owner} · edited {date}[ · {excerpt}]", *Private* `neutral` / *Shared* / *Shared with you* `info` badges, *See all in Notes* to `/notes?q=`). Autosave never toasts; it uses `SaveStatusComponent`. No other new component.
