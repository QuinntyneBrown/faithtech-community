Activity feed

A newest-first stream of things that happened, each with the actor's avatar, a sentence and a time: Recent activity on Home, comments on a story, and the history on a partner.

Overview

Use a feed for events in time order where the reader wants to know who did what and when: Tomás Ortega signing up, Marcus Lee publishing Toronto Builders Night, a comment on the "Tech for Shelters hackathon" story.

  • A feed sits inside a card with a heading ("Recent activity", "Comments (2)", "History").
  • Use a list for things to open, and the notifications list for items with read and unread state.
  • Name the actor in full in every feed ("Grace Okonkwo moved Kestrel Labs…", "Marcus Lee: …"), as Home does; never first names only.
  • In Angular, use <ft-feed> and <ft-feed-item>. ft-feed takes state (ready, loading, empty, error) and, for errors, retrying and a (retry) output.

Anatomy

  1. ActorSmall avatar of whoever acted, top-aligned.
  2. Body (.feed__body)One sentence; the object is a link when it can be opened.
  3. Meta (.feed__meta)Time in --text-caption, muted; on comments, "Author · date".

Items have no hover or selected state; only links inside them are interactive. The card keeps its "Recent activity" heading in every state. Loading is a busy list of skeleton rows (pages/home/loading); empty is one muted line (pages/home/empty); error is a danger alert with a Try again <button> while the rest of the page stays (pages/home/activity-error). When the whole page fails, the page-level error state replaces every card (pages/home/error).

Responsive behaviour

  • On Home the feed is in the side column at desktop and drops below "Coming up" on a phone.
  • Sentences wrap beside the avatar, which stays top-aligned and never shrinks.

Theming

Dividers, body and meta use border and foreground tokens and follow the theme; avatars keep their person colours.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <ul> inside a section labelled by its heading (aria-labelledby). New items are not announced live; the feed refreshes on navigation.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves between links in the items.
EnterOpens the linked event, contact or story.

Focus

The shared focus ring on inline links.

Labelling

Avatars are hidden; the sentence or meta names the actor. Link text names the object ("Toronto Builders Night"), and short follow-ups like "Review" sit right after the sentence that gives them context.

Content

  • Sentence: actor, past-tense verb, object. "Hannah Kim sent “November hackathon: teams are forming”".
  • Times: "Today, 11:42 am", "Yesterday, 4:10 pm", then "Mon 20 Oct".
  • Respect visibility: for Staff, a private contact's sign-up reads "Tomás Ortega joined FaithTech Community." with no hint of the contact.
