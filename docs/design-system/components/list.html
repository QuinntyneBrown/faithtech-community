Component · Data display

List

A stack of rows, one per person, invite or event, each with a leading avatar or icon, a title, a muted meta line and an optional trailing badge. Contacts, Team, Invites, Archived contacts and the event lists are all lists.

Overview

Use a list when each item is one thing with a name and a line of context, and the reader scans down to open one. Grace Okonkwo's private contacts, the Toronto team, pending invites, search results and "Coming up" on Home are lists.

  • Use a table instead when readers compare the same columns across rows (Admin users, audit log, roles).
  • Use a feed for things that happened, newest first, with a time on each.
  • In Angular, use <ft-list> and <ft-list-item> from the components library.

Anatomy

  1. LeadingA small avatar for a person, a sticker icon for an invite, or a date badge for an event.
  2. Title (.list__title)The name, as a link when the row opens a detail page.
  3. Meta (.list__meta)One muted line of context, parts joined with " · ". .list__meta.cluster.cluster--tight holds role badges 4px apart.
  4. Trailing (optional)A status badge or a button.

Keep track of people you meet. Contacts and your notes on them are private to you until they join.
  • Loading: skeleton rows of the same shape, the container marked aria-busy="true" with a label such as "Loading contacts".
  • Empty, no results and error replace the list with an empty state ("No contacts yet", "No contacts match “harbour”", "We couldn’t load your contacts").
  • Hover: a row whose title is a link tints to --color-bg-subtle; rows without a link do not change.
  • Focus: the row is never focusable. Focus lands on the title link (or a trailing button), which shows the shared focus ring; the row does not tint on focus.
  • Rows have no selected or disabled state; archived contacts move to their own list.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of its column at every breakpoint; the leading avatar and trailing badge keep their size and the body takes the rest (min-width: 0).
  • On a phone the meta line wraps; it is never truncated, so a contact note stays readable.

Theming

Rows sit on the card surface; dividers use --color-border-default, the meta line --color-fg-muted, a hovered link row --color-bg-subtle. Both follow the theme with no list-specific tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <ul> with <li> rows, so screen readers announce "list, 4 items". The only interactive element in a row is the title link (or a trailing button); the whole row is not a click target.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the next row's title link or trailing button.
EnterOpens the contact, profile or event.

Focus

The shared focus ring on the title link.

Labelling

Avatars and stickers are aria-hidden; the title names the row. A badge's text is read as part of the row ("Private"); the lock icon in it is hidden.

Content

  • Title: the person's full name, the invite email or the event name.
  • Meta: the most useful fact first, then " · ": "Pastor at Maple Street Church · interested in hosting".
  • Dates use "Mon 20 Oct"; today and yesterday are written as words.
  • Badges are one or two words in sentence case: "Signed up", "Pending".
