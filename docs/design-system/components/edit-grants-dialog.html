Edit grants dialog

Who may grant: one checkbox per active role under “Holders of {role} may add and remove:”, administration disabled for a grantor that lacks it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: designed, not builtCDK Dialog

Overview

The grants dialog sets which roles a holder of one role may add and remove. It is a single-column capability checklist of every non-archived role. A role carrying Administer the organisation is disabled with its reason unless the grantor carries it too. When the grantor lacks Assign roles, an info alert explains that the rules are stored but inactive.

Anatomy

  1. Header“Who may grant: {role}” and a description.
  2. Checklist“Holders of {role} may add and remove:” with each role and its description.
  3. Disabled entryAdministration roles, disabled with “Only administrators can grant administration”.
  4. FooterCancel, Save rules.

Variants

Who may grant: City Lead

Grant rules decide which roles a holder of this role may add to people and remove from them.

Holders of City Lead may add and remove:

Holders of Administer the organisation can add and remove every role without a rule.

Default: City Lead may grant six roles.

Who may grant: Staff

Grant rules decide which roles a holder of this role may add and remove.

This role can’t assign roles until it has the Assign roles capability. The rules are saved and start working when it does.
Grantor without Assign roles.

Sizes

Not applicable. A bottom sheet below 40rem and a centred panel above, as every dialog.

States

  • Invalid: a 400 for an administration role shows a field error under that entry.
  • Busy: every checkbox disabled, “Saving…”.
  • Failed: “Grant rules not saved”, input kept, “Try again”.
  • Success: toast “Grant rules saved”.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 40rem it is a bottom sheet with the footer pinned; the body scrolls.
  • From 40rem it is a centred panel; see Dialog.

Theming

Raised surface --color-bg-surface-raised on the backdrop in both themes, as every dialog.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA modal dialog through Angular CDK Dialog: focus trapped, the page behind inert, Esc closes unless busy, focus returns to the button that opened it.

KeyAction
TabMoves through fields, checkboxes and footer buttons.
EscCloses without saving (not while busy).
Enter on the primary buttonSaves.

Content

  • The legend always names the grantor: “Holders of City Lead may add and remove:”.
  • The closing caption reminds that administrators need no rule.

Do and don't

Tokens

The dialog tokens.

Code

In Angular: <ft-edit-grants-dialog> (EditGrantsDialogComponent, data: grantor, roles, chosen, grantorCanAssign, grantorIsAdministrator, submit), opened through DialogService (ADR 0005). Its submit callback resolves to null on success or to a message the dialog shows (a 400 field error, a 403 or 409 reason, or the failure with Try again).
