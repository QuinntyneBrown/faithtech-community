Component · Navigation

Tab bar

Navigation on a phone: Home, Events, Search, Contacts and Partners pinned to the bottom of the screen, within thumb reach. From 768px it hides and the top bar nav takes over.

Overview

Use the tab bar on every signed-in page. It is the phone form of the top bar: the five places a member goes most, built by one rule from their roles. Search takes a tab because the top bar search field only appears from 1024px. The last tab, More, is a button that opens the Main menu drawer (.nav-drawer) listing every section the member may open, so Partners, Workspaces, Team, Announcements, People and Admin stay one tap away. The auth pages and the public event page have no tab bar.

  • Tabs are links, not ARIA tabs: each one goes to a different page.
  • The page wrapper gets .has-tabbar so the last content is not hidden behind the bar.
  • In these docs, .tabbar--static renders the bar in the flow of the page at every width.

Anatomy

  1. Barnav.tabbar, fixed to the bottom, --layout-tabbar-height (64px) plus the safe-area inset, surface background, top hairline.
  2. Icon24px, aria-hidden.
  3. LabelOne word in --text-caption, medium weight; the accessible name.
  4. Current tabaria-current="page": icon and label in --color-fg-accent.

Variants

One rule for every role (ADR 0009): Home, Events, Search, the first of the member's other sections, then More, in the order Contacts, Partners, Workspaces, Team, Announcements, People, Admin. Contacts are for leads and System Admin; Partners for leads, Staff and System Admin; Workspaces for leads, Volunteers, Staff and System Admin; Team and Announcements for everyone. So a lead or System Admin gets Contacts, Staff get Partners, a Volunteer gets Workspaces, and a member with no role gets Team. Every other section is in More. The app builds the bar once, in AppShellComponent.

Sizes

One height, --layout-tabbar-height (64px) plus env(safe-area-inset-bottom); tabs share the width equally.

  • Current uses colour and aria-current="page"; it is set on the section's tab for every page under it (Contacts stays current on a contact, a new contact and Archived contacts).
  • None current: pages outside the tabbed sections (Workspaces for members, Team, Announcements, People, your profile, Settings) show the bar with no current tab.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below --layout-breakpoint-md (768px): fixed to the bottom at --z-sticky; .has-tabbar adds bottom padding equal to the bar plus the safe-area inset.
  • From 768px: hidden (display: none), and the top bar nav shows. The two never show together.
  • At 360px five tabs are 72px each; labels are single words, so nothing truncates.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-surface, a hairline --color-border-default on top, --color-fg-muted for tabs and --color-fg-accent (violet) for the current tab. Inherited from Shelfie unchanged; the dark theme changes only those semantic tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <nav aria-label="Primary, phone"> of links, distinct from the top bar's "Primary" nav. Not the ARIA tabs pattern: each tab loads a page.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabReaches the tabs after the page content, in order.
EnterFollows the link.

Focus

The shared 2px --color-focus-ring, inset so it is not clipped by the screen edge.

Labelling

The visible word is the name; icons are aria-hidden. Current is announced through aria-current="page", not colour alone.

Motion

Colour change over --duration-fast; reduced motion zeroes it.

Touch

Each tab is the full bar height (64px) and at least 72px wide at 360px, well above the 44px comfortable target.

Content

  • One word per tab, same as the top bar: Home, Events, Search, Contacts, Partners. Always in that order.
  • Leave out a tab the person may not use; never show it disabled. Staff get Workspaces in place of Contacts.
