Person summary

A centred avatar, name and one line of meta: who a visitor is about to connect with on the connect page.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: new

Overview

The profile header is for a member's own page (left-aligned, roles as badges, photo strip). On an auth card the visitor needs only who the person is, centred above the form: the person summary.

Anatomy

  1. AvatarThe avatar at xl with the featured photo, initials and colour from the profile id (ADR 0008) when there is none.
  2. Name--text-h4.
  3. MetaRole and city, "City Lead · Toronto", --text-body-sm muted. Omitted parts drop their separator.

Variants

One variant.

Sizes

Avatar xl on auth cards, lg in dialogs.

GO

Grace Okonkwo

City Lead · Toronto

States

Two states: loaded, and loading (skeleton circle and two text lines, see skeleton).

Responsive behaviour

Centred at every width; long names wrap.

Theming

Inherits avatar and text tokens in both themes.

Accessibility

  • The avatar is decorative (aria-hidden) because the name is printed next to it.
  • The name is text, not a heading; the page owns its h1.

Content

Meta reads "Role · City". Show the highest lead role the person holds.
