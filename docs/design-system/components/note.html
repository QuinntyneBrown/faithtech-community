Note

A note keeps what a lead learned about a contact — where they met and what they care about — visible only to that lead and System Admin.

Overview

A note is one dated, private annotation a lead wrote about a contact: where they met, what the person cares about, what to follow up. Notes sit in the "Your notes" card on a contact, newest first, below the Add a note form.

  • Use it only for private contact notes (pages/contact). Notes are visible only to the lead who wrote them and to System Admin; the server enforces this on every query and in search.
  • Notes stay private when the contact signs up and is linked to a member profile (pages/contact/linked).
  • For quoted announcements or comments on work items use a feed or card, not a note.

Anatomy

  1. Accent ruleLeft border, --space-1 wide, --color-accent. Decorative.
  2. BodyOne or more paragraphs of plain text on --color-bg-subtle.
  3. Meta.note__meta: the date written, caption size, muted.

One variant. The surrounding section always says the notes are private.

Responsive behaviour

  • Notes fill the main column of the contact's .split layout; on a phone that column is full width and Details moves below.
  • Text wraps; long URLs or emails break with the normal flow. Nothing truncates.

Theming

Reads --color-bg-subtle, --color-accent and --color-fg-muted. The accent rule follows the brand accent in both themes; the background darkens in dark mode.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain <div> with paragraphs, inside a <section aria-labelledby> whose heading contains "Private" as text. The privacy is said in words, not shown only by the lock icon.

Keyboard

Notes are not focusable. The add form's textarea and button are in DOM order before the notes.

Labelling

The textarea has a visible label, "Add a note (optional)". The lock icon is aria-hidden. The accent rule is decorative.

Contrast

Body on note

Motion

None.

Content

  • Write for your future self: who, where you met, what they want. "Met at Builders Night. Interested in a data dashboard for bed availability."
  • Meta is the date only, in the "Thu 16 Oct" format. Author is implied: every note on a contact is yours.
  • Keep facts like email and phone in Details, not in notes.
  • Empty: "No notes yet. Only you will see what you write here."
