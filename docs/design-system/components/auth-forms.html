Sign-in and sign-up forms

The shared presentation of the sign-in and sign-up forms, used by the community app's /sign-in and /sign-up pages and by the marketing page's panel, with the Google social button above an "or" divider.

Overview

ft-sign-in-form and ft-sign-up-form live in the components library so both apps render the same fields, validation messages, alerts and busy states. They make no HTTP calls: the page passes busy, failure and server field errors in and handles submitted. A form that shows the Google button projects it into the [ftAuthAlternatives] slot and sets divider="or"; keepSignedIn() reads the switch for the other ways in.

Anatomy

  1. Alternatives (optional): the Google button and an "or" divider.
  2. Failure alert: incorrect credentials, locked, suspended, unreachable, Google failed.
  3. Fields: email and password (sign-in) or name, email and password (sign-up).
  4. Keep me signed in switch (sign-in).
  5. Submit: block, large, busy while submitting.
  6. Links: forgot password; join or sign in.

Variants

Google button (variant="google" on ft-button)

Follows Google's sign-in branding: the full-colour G mark at its own size, "Sign in with Google" or "Sign up with Google", Google's white fill and grey outline in light, dark fill in dark. The colours are the only brand tokens (--color-social-google-*).

Sizes

The forms fill their card; the card is at most 26rem (auth layout) or 28rem (hero panel).

States

Default, invalid (inline errors, summary for sign-up, focus moves to the first error), busy (submit or Google button busy and inert), incorrect credentials, locked, suspended, unreachable, Google failed and sign-up success (check your inbox). See the marketing mocks and sign-in mocks for each state.

Responsive behaviour

Single column at every width; buttons are full width.

Theming

Inherits the field, button and alert tokens; the Google button switches between Google's light and dark fills.

Accessibility

  • Every field has a visible label; errors are linked with aria-describedby and announced.
  • On a failed submit focus moves to the first error or the alert.
  • The G mark is decorative (alt=""); the button text names it.
  • The Google button keeps at least 3:1 contrast for its outline in both themes.

Content

  • "Sign in with Google" on the sign-in form, "Sign up with Google" on the join form; both start the same flow.
  • Error copy matches the community app's sign-in and sign-up pages word for word.
