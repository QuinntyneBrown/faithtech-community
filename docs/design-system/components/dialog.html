Dialog

A focused task on top of the page: send an invite, add a role, archive a contact, cancel an event. It holds focus until it is done or cancelled, and becomes a bottom sheet on phones.

Overview

Use a dialog for a short task that belongs to the page underneath and must be finished or cancelled before going on. Button-triggered editing in FaithTech Community always opens a dialog or a new screen, never an inline form. The mocks have 23 dialogs in two kinds:

  • Form dialogs: Send invite, Add a role, Add a partner, Move partner, New workspace, Add members, New work item, Start a sprint, Start a Planning Poker session, Choose a new owner. A neutral icon, a title, a description and a few fields.
  • Confirmations: Archive contact, Delete contact, Delete photo, Remove role, Suspend user, Cancel event, Cancel RSVP, Archive workspace, Remove member, Archive work item, Close sprint, Switch method, Link contact. The title is a question; a danger button confirms when something is removed or hidden. Archive workspace and Remove member add the dialog__icon--danger circle.

Do not use a dialog for messages (use a toast or alert) or for navigation. The account menu is a menu, not a dialog.

Anatomy

  1. Backdrop and frame.backdrop dims the page with --color-bg-backdrop at --z-overlay; .dialog (role="dialog", aria-modal="true") centres the panel at --z-modal.
  2. Icon.dialog__icon: 40px neutral circle naming the task (send, trash, archive). .dialog__icon--danger tints it for every destructive confirmation, the ones whose main button is danger (Delete contact, Delete photo, Suspend user, Cancel event, Archive workspace, Remove member). Reversible confirmations keep the neutral icon.
  3. Title and description.dialog__heading wraps h2.dialog__title (via aria-labelledby) and p.dialog__description (muted, via aria-describedby). --text-h4 on phones, --text-h3 from 640px.
  4. CloseGhost icon button, aria-label="Close".
  5. Body.dialog__body, usually a .stack of fields; scrolls inside the panel when tall. Optional: a confirmation whose title and description say everything (Archive contact, Archive workspace, Cancel RSVP) leaves it out, and .dialog__header + .dialog__footer keeps the gap. Never fill it with placeholder or visually hidden text.
  6. Footer.dialog__footer: sticky, right-aligned, Cancel first, primary or danger last.

Variants

In-page examples use .dialog--static, a documentation hook that renders the dialog in flow. They carry role="dialog" but not aria-modal, so this page stays readable; the product sets both.

Log and add: activity and walk-ins

dialogs/log-activity is a form dialog: a type radio row (Call, Email, Meeting, Note), "What happened" (required, with a "105 / 2000" counter), Date (required, not in the future) and an optional Time in a .grid--pair, an optional Next step (counter "24 / 200", help "Shown on the partner card so any lead can pick it up.") and an optional Next step due date. The confirm button follows the type ("Log call"). dialogs/check-in-walk-in takes a name and an optional email, and its "Check them in now" switch is on by default. dialogs/restore-partner (System Admin, from the archived partner) is a plain confirm: "Restore Kestrel Labs Inc.?", a description of what comes back ("its old stage, Lead, and the timeline entries that came from it"), a muted line that the merge choices stay on the kept record, then Cancel and "Restore partner" (busy "Restoring…"; failed "Partner not restored"). It is not destructive, so it has no danger icon. Log activity and Add walk-in follow the form-dialog states: invalid shows a field error ("Say what happened, in a sentence or two."), busy disables the form, and failed keeps the input and shows a danger alert.

Sizes

One panel width, up to 32rem; height grows with content up to the viewport minus --space-12, then the body scrolls and the footer stays.

States

The mocks give every dialog default, busy and failed; dialogs with input also have invalid. The controls inside use their own real attributes.

Invalid

Failed

Invite not sent
We couldn’t reach FaithTech. Nothing was changed — try again.
  • Default: focus on the first field (form dialogs) or on Cancel (confirmations).
  • Busy: Cancel and Close take native disabled (focus is not on them, so they leave the tab order); the pressed button keeps focus with aria-busy="true" and aria-disabled="true", always both, and a spinner over its busy label ("Sending invite…"). Text fields and textareas become readonly (they keep their value and stay readable); selects, radios and checkboxes, which have no read-only state, become disabled. Escape does nothing until it settles.
  • Invalid: the field gets aria-invalid="true" and a .field__error linked with aria-describedby; focus moves to it. The dialog stays open. Examples: "Enter an email like name@example.org.", "Give a reason so other admins understand."
  • Failed: a danger alert with role="alert" at the top of the body; everything typed stays. The title names what was not saved, in the form "Thing not done" ("Invite not sent", "Role not removed", "Contact not deleted", "Account not suspended"); the text says why and what to do: "We couldn’t reach FaithTech. Nothing was changed — try again." Never a generic "That didn’t work".

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm) the panel is a bottom sheet: full width, pinned to the bottom, top corners --radius-xl, sliding up over --duration-slow.
  • From 640px it is centred with --space-6 around it, all corners --radius-lg, scaling in; the title grows to --text-h3.
  • The footer is sticky, so the actions stay in reach when the body scrolls or the phone keyboard is up. Buttons wrap, primary last.

Bottom sheet at 360px: rounded top corners only, full width. Real pages get this from the base styles, no modifier needed.

Theming

The panel is --color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-4, over --color-bg-backdrop. In the dark theme the raised surface is lighter than the page, so the panel still lifts off a dark backdrop; the backdrop itself gets darker. The neutral icon reads the brand subtle tokens; the danger icon reads --color-danger-bg and --color-danger-icon.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The WAI-ARIA APG Dialog (Modal) pattern: role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby pointing at the title and aria-describedby at the description. The page behind is inert while it is open. In the app this is the Angular CDK Dialog; never hand-roll it.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the controls inside and wraps from the last to the first; focus never leaves the dialog.
EscCancels and closes (same as Cancel). Ignored while busy.
Enter, SpaceActivates the focused button; Enter in a single-line field submits.
↑ ↓Moves between radios (Remove role, Move partner, Start a sprint) and select options.

Focus

On open, focus goes to the first field (Email, Reason, Message to attendees) or, for confirmations, to Cancel so a stray Enter is safe. Mocks mark it with autofocus; in the app, set initialFocus on that ft-button, ft-text-field, ft-textarea or ft-radio (Send invite sets it on Email, Add role on the first role). Without it the Close button, first in the dialog, would take focus. On close, focus returns to the button that opened it (Invite, Remove role, Archive). On invalid, focus moves to the first invalid field; on failed, the alert is announced and focus stays where it was.

Labelling

The title names the dialog and the subject: "Invite someone to FaithTech", "Suspend Leah Thompson?". The close button is aria-label="Close". Icons are aria-hidden; the title and the danger button's words carry the warning.

Motion

The backdrop fades in over --duration-base; the sheet slides up (phones) or the panel scales in from 96% (wider) over --duration-slow with --ease-enter. Reduced motion zeroes both, so it simply appears; the busy dot stops pulsing.

Touch

Footer buttons are 44px tall with 12px between them; the close button is 44px square. The sheet keeps the actions at the bottom, in thumb reach.

Content

  • Form titles say the task: "Invite someone to FaithTech", "Add a role for Jonah Bell", "Start a Planning Poker session".
  • Confirmation titles are a question naming the person or thing: "Archive Tomás Ortega?", "Cancel Toronto Builders Night?".
  • The description says what happens next and whether it can be undone: "You can restore him whenever you like." or "It can’t be undone."
  • Buttons are specific verbs: "Send invite", "Remove role", "Archive workspace", "Delete permanently". Never "OK" or "Yes". When the action itself is "Cancel …", name the safe button differently ("Keep event", "Keep my spot").
  • Admin actions that are audited say so in the label: "Reason (recorded in the audit log)".

Role dialog (RoleDialogComponent)

The add-role and remove-role dialogs are one library component, ft-role-dialog, opened with DialogService from any screen (ADR 0005). Data: mode (add or remove), name, description, options (value, label, optional description), optional caption and a submit(value) callback that resolves to null on success or to a message to show. Add mode titles "Add a role for {name}", needs a choice ("Choose a role to add.") and confirms with a primary Add role. Remove mode titles "Remove {role} from {name}?", preselects the first role and confirms with a danger Remove role. Both show the busy and failed states above and close with the chosen role.
