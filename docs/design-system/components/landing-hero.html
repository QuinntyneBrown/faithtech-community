Landing hero

The marketing page's split hero: eyebrow, headline and lead beside the sign-in panel at LG and wider, with the media below the copy; stacked copy, panel, media on smaller screens.

Overview

ft-landing-hero lays out three slots: inputs for the eyebrow, heading and lead, a [ftHeroPanel] slot for the auth form card and a [ftHeroMedia] slot for the video. It takes its composition from a split product hero (headline left, form right) and keeps every value on tokens.

Two companions sit below the hero on the marketing page: ft-benefit (an icon tile, an <h3> and a sentence, laid out in an auto grid) and ft-cta-band (a named region with an <h2>, a line and one action, on the accent-subtle wash).

Anatomy

  1. Eyebrow: --text-overline in --color-fg-accent.
  2. Title: the page's only <h1>, --text-h1, --text-display from LG.
  3. Lead: --text-body-lg, muted, at most one measure wide.
  4. Panel: a card up to 28rem wide.
  5. Media: below the copy.

Variants

One variant.

Sizes

Fluid inside the container.

States

Meet the builders, leads and volunteers in your city.

Panel slot: sign-in or sign-up form

Media slot: video player

The hero itself has no states; its panel and media carry them.

Responsive behaviour

  • XS–MD: one column in the order copy, panel, media, so the form is reachable without scrolling past the video.
  • LG and wider: two columns 7:5; copy and media on the left, the panel spanning both rows on the right.

Theming

Text uses the default and muted foregrounds; nothing changes between themes beyond the tokens.

Accessibility

  • The title is the page's <h1>; the panel has an <h2>.
  • Source order equals visual order at every width, so focus order follows the reading order.

Content

  • One sentence headline, one sentence lead. No jargon.
