Site footer

The marketing page's footer: copyright on the left and a short list of links on the right, wrapping on small screens.

Overview

ft-site-footer closes the marketing page. It is a <footer> landmark with the copyright and up to four links. The community app has no footer.

Anatomy

  1. Rule: hairline top border.
  2. Copyright.
  3. Links: a list, muted, at least the 24px target height.

Variants

One variant.

Sizes

Full width inside the container.

States

© 2026 FaithTech

  • Join now
  • Sign in

© 2026 FaithTech

  • Join now
  • Sign in

Responsive behaviour

Copyright and links sit on one line from SM; below that they wrap.

Theming

Muted text and hairline border tokens.

Accessibility

  • One <footer> landmark; links are a list and underlined.
  • Muted text keeps 4.5:1 on the canvas.

Content

  • "© 2026 FaithTech", then actions in sentence case.

Do and don't

Tokens

PartToken
Border--color-border-default
Text--color-fg-muted
Padding--space-6
Link target--target-min

Code

<ft-site-footer copyright="© 2026 FaithTech">
  <a href="#join">Join now</a>
</ft-site-footer>
