Form layout

The frame around a form: the button row at the end, the error summary after a failed submit, and the danger zone for the one action that cannot be taken back lightly.

Overview

Form layout is the frame around a form's fields: the row of buttons at the end, the error summary at the top after a failed submit, and the danger zone below the form for actions that cannot be undone casually.

  • Form actions (.form-actions): the primary submit and a way out, at the end of every page form (event form, contact form, settings, compose announcement, edit profile).
  • Error summary (.form-summary): a danger alert listing every problem as a link to its field, shown and focused when a submit fails.
  • Danger zone (.danger-zone): a red-bordered section after the form for one destructive action: "Sign out everywhere" in Settings, "Archive workspace" in workspace settings.

A page form is a form.stack capped at 44 to 48rem; long forms group fields in section.card blocks with an h2 ("Account", "Email me when").

Anatomy

  1. Error summaryDanger alert with a --space-1 left edge, a count in the title and one link per error.
  2. Form actionsA wrapping row, --space-3 gap, --space-4 above.
  3. Danger zone--color-danger-border hairline, --radius-lg, --space-6 padding; heading and consequence on the left, danger button on the right.

Archive workspace
  • Invalid: on submit the summary appears above the first field, takes focus (tabindex="-1", role="alert") and each field shows its own error.
  • Busy: the primary shows the loading state; Cancel stays a link. Fields stay editable.
  • Success: the page either navigates to the result (the new event, the contact) or, in Settings, shows a success alert at the top.
  • Error (the server failed) uses a danger alert in the same place as the summary, without field links: see alert.

Responsive behaviour

  • Form actions wrap; on a phone the buttons keep their order and natural width.
  • The danger zone wraps its button below the text when the row is narrower than both (flex-wrap).
  • Forms keep the 16px side gutter at 360px; the 44 to 48rem cap only matters on wide screens.

Theming

The summary inherits the danger alert's tokens (--color-danger-bg, --color-danger-fg, --color-danger-icon) and adds a --space-1 wide --color-danger-solid left edge. The danger zone uses --color-danger-border and --radius-lg on the page background, so it reads as a warning without filling with red.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The summary is role="alert" with tabindex="-1" and receives focus after a failed submit (GOV.UK error summary pattern). The danger zone is a section labelled by its heading. Form actions need no role.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab from the summaryMoves through the error links in order.
Enter on an error linkMoves focus to that field.
Enter in a fieldSubmits through the primary.

Focus

The summary shows the focus ring when focused programmatically, so sighted keyboard users see where they are. After success, focus moves to the success alert or the new page's heading.

Labelling

Error links use the same words as the field errors. The danger zone's aria-labelledby points at its heading, so it appears in the landmarks list as "Sign out everywhere".

Contrast

Summary text on danger background
Summary edge on page
Danger zone border on page

Motion

None. The summary appears without animation so it is never missed under reduced motion.

Touch

Buttons in form actions are 44px tall and --space-3 apart.

Content

  • Summary titles count and name the action: "Fix 1 thing to save", "Fix 2 things to send", "Fix 3 things to publish".
  • One summary line per problem, short and imperative: "Enter a title", "Add a subject".
  • Danger zone headings are the action ("Archive this workspace"); the line under says what happens and whether it can be undone ("You can restore it from Workspaces.").
  • The way out is "Cancel", never "Back" or "Discard".
