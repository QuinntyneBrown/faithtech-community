Menu

A short list of actions about the signed-in person, opened from the avatar in the top bar on every signed-in page.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAPG Menu Button

Overview

The account menu opens from the avatar at the right of the top bar and holds the few things that are about the signed-in person, not the page: their profile, their settings and signing out of this device.

Every signed-in mock opens it from button.avatar-trigger; in a static mock the panel is a native popover (.menu--account) so it opens without script. "Sign out" ends the session on this device only; "Sign out everywhere" stays in Settings. The admin area is a top-bar section for System Admins, not a menu item.

  • Use a menu for a short list (two to six) of navigation or commands that belong together and would crowd the top bar.
  • Do not use it for choosing a value in a form; use a radio group or select. Do not use it for page navigation; use the top bar nav.

Anatomy

  1. Triggerbutton.avatar-trigger wrapping the initials avatar, with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; 32px in the top bar.
  2. Identity headerWho is signed in: name, then roles and city. Not an item: role="none", never focused.
  3. Divider.menu__divider, aria-hidden="true"; separates the header and puts Sign out on its own.
  4. Item.menu__item: icon plus label, 36px tall, full width.

Variants

The header changes with who is signed in; the items are the same for everyone. Items that do not apply are left out, not disabled.

  • Open sets aria-expanded="true" on the trigger and shows the panel below its right edge.
  • Disabled (aria-disabled="true") exists in the CSS but is not used: leave out an item the person cannot use rather than greying it.
  • There is no loading or error state. Sign out is a link to the sign-in page; any failure shows there.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel is anchored to the trigger's right edge and is at least 12rem wide, so it fits a 360px phone with the 16px gutter.
  • The top bar keeps the avatar at every width; under 640px the nav moves to the tab bar but the menu stays.
  • Items are 36px tall; on touch the whole row is the target.

Theming

The panel reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default and --shadow-3; items read --color-fg-default and --color-bg-subtle for hover and focus. In dark the raised surface is lighter than the page, so elevation shows without relying on the shadow. The trigger's hover ring is --color-border-strong.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Menu Button pattern. The trigger is a native <button> with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at the menu. The list is role="menu", each <li> is role="none" and each link is role="menuitem". Focus inside the menu is roving: only the current item has tabindex="0". In the app this is the Angular CDK Menu.

Keyboard

KeyAction

↑ on the triggerOpens the menu and focuses the last item (Sign out).
↓ / ↑Moves to the next or previous item, wrapping at the ends. The header and dividers are skipped.
Home / EndMoves to the first or last item.

TabCloses the menu and moves focus to the next element after the trigger.

Focus

The trigger shows the shared focus ring. Items show focus with the --color-bg-subtle fill plus the shared focus ring drawn inside the item, and the menu moves real DOM focus (not aria-activedescendant). A click outside closes the menu without moving focus.

Labelling

The trigger's aria-label names whose menu it is ("Account menu for Grace Okonkwo"), because the visible "GO" says little. The menu repeats it with aria-label. The identity header is plain text inside role="none", so it is not announced as an item; icons are aria-hidden.

Motion

The panel scales in from the top right over --duration-fast with --ease-enter. Reduced motion zeroes the duration, so it simply appears.

Touch

The 32px avatar trigger clears the 24px minimum; items are 36px tall and full width.

Content

  • Item labels are short nouns or verbs in sentence case: "Your profile", "Settings", "Sign out".
  • The header shows the full name, then roles and city separated by a middle dot: "City Lead · Toronto". A person with no role reads "No role yet".
  • Sign out is always last, after a divider.
