Component · Containers

Card

A raised surface that holds one group of things: the events coming up, the partner pipeline counts, Grace's recent activity. A success card confirms that something worked, an RSVP saved or an announcement sent.

Overview

Use a card to group content that belongs together and has its own title. Cards are not clickable: actions inside a card are real buttons and links. The component is inherited from Shelfie unchanged.

  • Card (.card, usually with .stack): 24px padding. Profile sections, event details, stats, forms.
  • Flush (.card--flush): no padding, clips its content. Wraps a list or table edge to edge, such as "Coming up" on Home. A closing link or caption goes in .card__footer (<ft-card-footer>).
  • Stat strip (.card.cluster.stat-row.card--strip): a row of stats 32px apart, spaced 16px from the board below, as in the team board sprint summary (<ft-card variant="strip">).
  • Success (.card--success): a green tint fading into the surface, with role="status". Confirms a finished task: "You’re on the list, Abby", "Sent to 41 Product Engineers".
  • Title (.card__title) and stats (.stat) go inside.
  • Header action: one button may sit beside the title, as in workspace settings "Members (5) · Add members" (ftCardActions in Angular).

Anatomy

  1. Surface--color-bg-surface, hairline --color-border-default, --radius-lg, --shadow-1, --space-6 padding.
  2. Title.card__title in --text-h4 on a real heading (h2 or h3). In a header row beside a link or badge, use .section-title, the same role without the margin.
  3. ContentUsually a .stack; here three .stats in a .cluster.

Sizes

One padding (--space-6), or none with .card--flush. Width comes from the layout it sits in.

States

Cards are not interactive, so they have no hover, focus or pressed state. What changes is their content: loading shows skeletons in the same card, and empty shows an empty state inside it.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill their column. On phones every card is full width and stacks; on wider screens they sit in .split or .grid columns (see Layout).
  • Padding stays 24px at every width so content lines up with the page header.
  • Content inside uses .cluster and wraps: the success card's two buttons stack on a phone.

Theming

The card reads one component token, --card-bg (default --color-bg-surface). The success variant draws a gradient from --color-success-bg into --card-bg, so its text always sits on the surface end. In the dark theme the surface darkens and --shadow-1 deepens; the hairline border keeps the edge visible.

.card { --card-bg: var(--color-bg-surface); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> when the card is a named region (it becomes a region landmark), an <article> for a self-contained item, a <form> when it is a form. A success card that appears after an action is a <section role="status" aria-labelledby> named by its heading, so it is announced with a name ("You’re on the list, Abby", "Sent to 41 Product Engineers"); <ft-card variant="success" heading="…"> does this in Angular.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabThe card is not focusable; focus moves through the links and buttons inside it.

Focus

.card--flush clips its content (overflow: hidden); controls inside keep the 2px ring visible because list rows are padded by --space-4.

Labelling

The .card__title is a real heading at the right level and names the section through aria-labelledby. The success tint is decorative: the heading words carry the meaning.

Contrast

The card edge is decorative and not required to meet 3:1.

Motion

None.

Touch

The card itself is not a target. Targets inside follow their own rules (44px buttons, 24px minimum).

Content

  • Titles are short and specific: "Coming up", "Partner pipeline", "Recent activity", "Photos".
  • Success cards say what happened and to whom ("Sent to 41 Product Engineers", "You’re on the list, Abby"), then offer the next step.
  • Stat labels use the domain words ("Lead", "In funnel", "Confirmed"); values are numbers.

ClassEffect
.cardSurface, border, radius, shadow, 24px padding.
.card--flushNo padding; clips content. Pair with .list or .table-wrap--flush.
.card--successSuccess tint fading into the surface. Replacing a form, it takes the form's width class (.form-width--md).
.card--stripWith .cluster.stat-row: a row of stats, 16px above the board.
.card__titleCard heading; padded inside .card--flush.
.card__footerLast row of a flush card, such as the "All events" link or a caption; padded --space-3 --space-4 to line up with list rows. <ft-card-footer> in Angular.
.stat, .stat__label, .stat__valueA labelled number (see Stat).

.card--warning and the automatic tint for a card holding a coloured tile are still in components.css (inherited from Shelfie), but no FaithTech mock uses them.
