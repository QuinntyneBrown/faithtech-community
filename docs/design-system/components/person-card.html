Person card

A person card shows one person in a grid, so a lead can recognise a face, scan who they are and open them. It replaces rows where seeing the person matters more than density.

Overview

A person card is one tile in a person grid: a decorative cover band, a large round photo (or initials) overlapping it, the name with a status badge, a two-line subtitle, a one-line secondary fact, a full-width View profile pill link and a round dismiss button on the cover. It is used on the contacts list (pages/contacts, L2-072, L2-073).

  • Use it when people choose a person by sight and the list is short enough to scan as tiles.
  • There is no Connect, Follow or Message action. The only call to action is View profile, a link to the person's page. The dismiss button is for an action the screen defines (Archive on contacts) and always confirms first.
  • Do not use it for dense or sortable lists; use the list or table. The team page, people directory, search results and archived contacts keep their rows (deferred).

Anatomy

  1. Cover band.person-card__cover: 80px (--size-cover-md) of the person's colour tint (--color-tint-*), aria-hidden. Decorative only; never an uploaded image (ADR 0017).
  2. Photo.avatar.person-card__photo: 120px circle (--size-cover-lg) with a surface-coloured ring, overlapping the band. The featured photo with its description as alt, or the person's initials on their avatar colour (aria-hidden).
  3. Name and badge.person-card__name: a heading, --text-h4, centred and wrapping; the status badge (Private, Signed up, Member) sits beside it.
  4. Subtitle.person-card__subtitle: muted body text, clamped to two lines with an ellipsis.
  5. Secondary line.person-card__secondary: one muted small line ("2 notes", "Added Thu 9 Oct").
  6. View profile.person-card__action .btn.btn--block: an outlined accent pill, full width, pinned to the bottom so rows line up.
  7. Dismiss.person-card__dismiss: 44px inverse circle with ×, top right of the cover. Named by its action ("Archive Tomás Ortega").

Cover tints

.person-card--sage|rose|sky|sun|lilac|mint|peach set --person-card-cover to the matching tint; without a modifier (plain) the band is --color-bg-subtle. On the contacts list the colour comes from the contact's id (ADR 0008, ADR 0017), so a person keeps it.

Sizes

One size. The grid column sets the width; the band, photo and pill keep fixed heights so every card in a row lines up.

States

The card lifts on hover (--shadow-2); the pill and the dismiss button have their own hover and focus. States are forced with data-state here.

Responsive behaviour

  • .person-grid: one column on phones, two from 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm, tablets), four from 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg, desktops).
  • Grid items stretch, and the card is a column flex box whose body grows, so every card in a row has the same height and the pills line up.
  • Names wrap; subtitles clamp to two lines and secondary lines to one, so a long organisation never stretches a row.

Theming

Only semantic tokens. The band reads --color-tint-*, which become the deep tints in the dark theme; the photo ring is --color-bg-surface; the pill uses the accent; the dismiss button is --color-bg-inverse with --color-fg-inverse, so it stays high-contrast on any band in either theme. No new tokens were needed.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <ul class="person-grid"> named by the page ("Contacts") of <li>s, each an <article> labelled by the name. The name is a heading (level 2 under the page title), so people can jump card to card.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabView profile, then the dismiss button, then the next card. The primary action comes first.
EnterOn View profile: opens the person's page. On dismiss: opens the confirmation dialog; focus returns to the dismiss button when it closes without archiving.
SpaceActivates the dismiss button.

Labelling

The pill reads "View profile Tomás Ortega" (the visible words come first, WCAG 2.5.3); the dismiss button reads "Archive Tomás Ortega". The photo's alt is the description its owner wrote; initials are aria-hidden. The status is said in words by the badge.

Contrast

Subtitle on surface
Pill text on surface
Pill border on surface
Dismiss × on its circle
Focus ring on surface

Motion

The hover shadow eases in --duration-fast; it is removed under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

The pill and the dismiss button are 44px high (--control-height-md), above the 24px minimum (WCAG 2.5.8), and sit apart.

Content

  • Name: as the lead saved it, or the member's own name once linked.
  • Subtitle: "{organisation} · {city}"; either part may be missing, and with neither the line is left out.
  • Secondary: "1 note" / "2 notes" when there are notes, otherwise "Added Thu 9 Oct".
  • Call to action: always "View profile". Never "Connect".

  [name]="contact.name"
  [photoSrc]="photo"            <!-- null shows the initials -->
  [photoAlt]="contact.photoAltText"
  [color]="colourFor(contact.id)"
  [subtitle]="'Harbour Light Shelter · Toronto'"
  [secondary]="'2 notes'"
  [href]="'/contacts/' + contact.id"
  dismissLabel="Archive Tomás Ortega"
  (dismissed)="archive(contact)">
  <ft-badge badge variant="success">Signed up</ft-badge>
</ft-person-card>
