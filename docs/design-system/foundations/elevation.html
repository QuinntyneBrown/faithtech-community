Foundation · Elevation

Elevation

Four shadow levels, a matching surface ladder and a fixed z-order, so menus, dialogs and toasts always stack the same way in both themes.

WCAG 2.2 AAFoundation

Overview

Elevation tells the reader what sits on top of what. FaithTech Community uses the four shadow levels inherited from Shelfie and a matching set of surface colours: the page canvas, cards on it, raised surfaces (menus and dialogs) above them, and the inverse toast on top. Stacking order is fixed by --z-* tokens so a menu never slides under the top bar.

  • Pair each shadow with its surface token; a raised surface is never just a darker shadow on a card colour.
  • Use the z-index tokens; never type a number.

Shadow levels

--shadow-1 · cards, Kanban cards
--shadow-2 · hovered clickable cards
--shadow-3 · menus, toasts
--shadow-4 · dialogs
--shadow-focus · focus halo
--shadow-1 · cards, Kanban cards

--shadow-3 · menus, toasts
--shadow-4 · dialogs

Shadows are built from --shadow-color (an RGB triplet) at increasing opacity. The dark theme sets the triplet to pure darkness and raises the opacities, because a soft grey shadow disappears on a dark canvas.

Elevation in the dark theme

  • Higher surfaces get lighter: canvas gray 950, surface gray 900, raised gray 800. The colour step does the work a shadow cannot.
  • Shadows stay, darker and more opaque, so edges still read on bright screens.
  • The inverse toast flips to a light surface, which is why its status icons have their own tokens (drift D2).
  • The backdrop darkens to 65% so the dialog stands clear of the page.

Usage

  • Cards sit at level 1. Do not raise a card to level 2 to make it "important"; use the hero or .card--success / .card--warning (drift D7).
  • Only one modal at a time; a dialog never opens another dialog.
  • Hover lifts are for things that move you somewhere (people rows, partner cards, planning poker estimate cards), not for static cards.
