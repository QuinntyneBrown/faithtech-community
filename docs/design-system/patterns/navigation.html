Pattern · Wayfinding

Navigation

The top bar is the same on every signed-in page: the brand, the main sections, global search, the bell and your avatar. On phones, a tab bar holds the five sections people use most. Inside a section, tabs switch views and breadcrumbs lead back up.

Overview

  • Top bar: FaithTech brand, then Home, Events, Contacts, Partners, Workspaces, Team, Announcements and People. Then search ("Search people, events, partners"), the bell and the avatar, which links to your profile.
  • Tab bar (under 768px): Home, Events, Search, Contacts, Partners.
  • Tabs (nav.tabs with aria-current): views inside one area. Admin has Users and Audit log; roles are assigned from People, which has its own top-bar link. A workspace has Board, Backlog, Planning Poker and Settings.
  • Breadcrumb: a way back up from detail and form pages, such as Contacts › New contact or Workspaces › Toronto team.
  • Skip link: "Skip to content", the first focusable element on every page.

Anonymous visitors see only public event pages. Those pages have the brand and a Sign in link, with no sections, search or bell.

When to use

NeedUse
Go to another sectionTop bar link, or the tab bar on phones
Find any person, event or partnerGlobal search in the top bar, or the Search tab on phones
Switch between views of the same thingTabs. Each tab is its own URL, so a view can be linked to.
Go back up from a detail or form pageBreadcrumb
Switch between two modes of one listView chips: links in a labelled <nav> with aria-current="page" on the current view, such as Tree and Sprint history in the backlog. Not tabs.

Role-aware destinations

Links point to everything the person may see. The server still checks every request. A Volunteer who is not in a workspace who opens a workspace link gets the forbidden screen. Staff see the same links as leads, and the pages are read-only for them.

  • Unread: the bell shows a count badge and its label says "Notifications, 3 unread". With nothing unread, the badge goes and the label is just "Notifications".

Between 768px and 1023px, Team, Announcements, People and Admin are hidden from the top bar; a More button at the end of the nav opens the Main menu drawer with every section, as the tab bar's More does on phones. Tabs scroll sideways without a scrollbar when they don't fit.

Accessibility

  • Each navigation region is a labelled <nav>: "Primary", "Primary, phone", "Breadcrumb" and "Admin sections" or "Workspace sections".
  • The current page is marked with aria-current="page" on nav links, tab bar items, tabs and the last breadcrumb item. Tabs that lead to other URLs are links in a nav, not ARIA tablist widgets.
  • The search form has role="search" and a visually hidden label, "Search FaithTech".
  • The avatar is a menu button named "Account menu for Grace Okonkwo"; its menu holds Your profile, Settings and Sign out. The bell's name includes the unread count.
  • "Skip to content" is the first element you can tab to, and it becomes visible on focus.
  • Tab bar targets are at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable).

Content

  • Section names are one plural noun: Events, Contacts, Partners, Workspaces, Announcements. "Home" is the exception.
  • Tab names say what the view is: Board, Backlog, Planning Poker, Settings, Audit log.
  • Breadcrumbs use the real names of things ("Toronto team"), not their type ("Workspace").
  • The search placeholder lists what can be found: "Search people, events, partners".

Do and don't

Sources

  • Top bar and tab bar: every signed-in page, for example home/default, contacts/default, bell/unread
  • Tabs: audit-log/default, admin-users/default, backlog/default, team-board/default
  • Breadcrumb: contact-form/default, compose-announcement/default, backlog/default
  • Anonymous: public-event/default; staff: home/staff; forbidden: forbidden/default

Drift noticed in the mocks:

  • Resolved: between 768px and 1023px the secondary sections were hidden with no "More" menu. The top bar now ends with More (.nav-link--more) at those widths.
  • Resolved: the tab bar had no way to reach Workspaces, Team or People on phones; its More tab now opens the Main menu.
  • Resolved: the tab rows no longer use an inline margin-bottom; .tabs carries the gap.
  • Resolved: the brand mark was an inline placeholder SVG with opacity=".35" in every mock; every top bar now shows the logo artwork (img.topbar__mark from assets/logo.svg), as the app's TopBarComponent does with ft-logo-mark.
