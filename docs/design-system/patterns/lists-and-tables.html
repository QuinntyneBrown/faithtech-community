Pattern · Collections

Lists and tables

Pick the layout that fits the question people ask of the data. Use a table to compare columns (who did what, and when). Use a list to scan people. Use a Kanban board to see stages. Use a tree to see what belongs to what.

Overview

  • Table (.table-wrap.card.card--flush > .table): the audit log, people and roles, admin users and invites.
  • List (.list.card.card--flush): contacts, archived contacts, team members, announcements and the notifications page (.notice-list).
  • Kanban (.kanban): the partner pipeline (Lead, In funnel, Confirmed) and the workspace board (To Do, In Progress, Done).
  • Tree (.tree): the backlog, with the hierarchy Initiative, Epic, Story, Task.
  • Event cards and search groups are lists too. They are covered on their own pages.

Every collection is filtered on the server by the viewer's permissions. Contacts come back only for the lead who owns them, or for System Admin. A Volunteer who is not in a workspace gets nothing back, not an empty board.

Responsive behaviour

  • Tables scroll sideways inside .table-wrap on narrow screens, so the page never scrolls sideways. Keep the first column short (name or time) so it stays readable.
  • Kanban is one column under 768px and three columns from 768px, for the partner pipeline and the workspace board alike. On phones the Move button replaces dragging.
  • Tree indents each level by --space-6, and rows wrap, so captions drop under the title on phones.
  • Lists keep the avatar and badge. The meta line wraps.

Accessibility

Semantics

Tables have a <caption> (it can be visually hidden) and scope="col" headers. An empty actions header still has a visually hidden name. Lists are <ul>. Kanban columns are labelled sections with a heading. The tree is nested lists inside <details>, so the browser announces expanded and collapsed. It is not an ARIA tree widget, so it needs no arrow-key handling.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through links and buttons, one row at a time.
Enter / Space on a summaryExpands or collapses a tree level.
Enter on MoveOpens the Move partner dialog. Dragging is never the only way to move a card.

Content

  • Headers are nouns: When, Who, What, Member, Roles, City.
  • Audit entries read as a sentence in the past tense and name the target: "Granted Staff to Daniel Reyes", "Suspended Victor Kane — reason: spam event listings".
  • Meta lines are short facts, joined with a middle dot: "Pastor at Maple Street Church · interested in hosting".
  • The subtitle gives the count and scope: "Private to you · 24 contacts", "Shared by every lead · 12 partners".
  • Missing values are written as words ("No role yet", "not estimated"), never left blank or shown as a dash.

Do and don't

Sources

  • Audit log: default, loading, empty, no-results, error; admin-users
  • People: default, admin, read-only, no-results, forbidden
  • Contacts: default, loading, empty, no-results, error; archived-contacts; team
  • Partners: default, dragging, read-only, empty; team-board, kanban
  • Backlog: default, sprints, empty

Drift noticed in the mocks:

  • Clusters in table cells use an inline style="gap: …"; there is no class for it. Filter selects take .field--sm, --md or --lg (12, 14, 16rem).
  • The loading skeleton list uses <div class="list__item"> outside a <ul>, with an inline flex:1.
  • In the backlog, the Task badge is class="badge badge--role ", with a trailing space and no tone.
