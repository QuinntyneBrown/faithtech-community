Pattern · Feedback

Notifications

FaithTech Community reaches people four ways. The bell keeps a list of what happened to you. A toast confirms what you just did. A banner states something that is still true. An email reaches you when you are not in the app.

Overview

  • Bell panel (.bell-panel with .notice-list): opens from the bell in the top bar and shows the latest four items. Unread items have a tinted row and a dot. "See all" opens the Notifications page, which uses the same .notice rows. Examples: "Tomás Ortega signed up — he’s one of your contacts", "Esther Nakamura cancelled Austin Faith & AI Panel", "Samuel Mensah assigned you Shelter problem briefs for teams".
  • Toast: a dark card in the bottom-right corner that confirms an action the person just took, such as "Invite sent", "You’re going" or "Contact restored". It usually has one action, Undo or Retry.
  • Banner (an .alert under the top bar): states something about the account that stays true across pages, such as "You’re viewing as Staff", "Verify your email to RSVP", "This account is suspended" or "Your email is verified".
  • Email: sent through SMTP or SendGrid for the same events as the bell (invites, RSVPs, cancellations, assignments, contacts who sign up, announcements). Announcement emails can be turned off. Account and activity emails are always sent.

For feedback on a field, a form or a part of the page, see feedback and loading.

Timing

  • Info and success toasts stay for at least 8 seconds. The timer pauses while the pointer is over the toast or focus is inside it. Undo works for as long as the toast is on screen.
  • Warning and danger toasts stay until they are dismissed or the retry works.
  • Banners stay while the condition is true and have no close button: Staff view, unverified email, suspended account. The "Your email is verified" success banner shows once, on the first page after verification.
  • Bell items stay unread until the person opens them or chooses "Mark all read". The badge counts unread items.
  • Only one banner shows at a time. If two apply, the one with the higher tone shows.

Toasts

All toasts appear in one region. The newest is added at the bottom. Each one has an icon, a title, one line of text, at most one action and a Dismiss button. Info and success toasts also show a timer bar; warning and danger toasts have none because they stay until dismissed.

Email

Every email has the same layout: a heading that says what happened, one sentence with the details, one button and a footer. Announcement emails end with a one-click unsubscribe link that works without signing in. Other emails explain that account and activity emails are always sent. The email is always shown in the light theme, because email clients do not use our tokens.

Email styling has one source: the .email classes in components.css, which read tokens by role and are always rendered inside data-theme="light". Email clients ignore stylesheets and custom properties, so the email build runs the template through a CSS inliner against tokens.css in the light theme; the hex colours, px sizes and font stack in the sent email come from that step. Nobody types them, in a mock or in a template.

Responsive behaviour

  • Bell: from 768px the panel drops down from the bell, up to 24rem wide (min(24rem, 100vw − 2 × --layout-margin)). On phones, the bell button goes straight to the Notifications page.
  • Toasts: on wide screens the region sits in the bottom-right corner. On phones it spans the bottom of the screen and sits above the tab bar, so it never covers the navigation.
  • Banners: full width under the top bar, inset by --layout-margin. On narrow screens the text and the action stack.
  • Email: one column, at most 36rem wide, so it reads well on a phone.

Accessibility

Roles

The bell button has aria-expanded and aria-controls, and its label includes the unread count ("Notifications, 3 unread"). The visible count badge is aria-hidden. The panel is a non-modal role="dialog" labelled "Notifications". The toast region is a polite live region that is on the page from the start. Danger toasts and the suspended banner use role="alert". "Unread" is written in the meta line, so the state does not depend on the tint or the dot.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter / Space on the bellOpens the panel and moves focus to its first link.
TabMoves through Mark all read, each notice link and See all.
EscCloses the panel and returns focus to the bell.
Tab to a toastPauses its timer. Enter runs Undo or Try again.

Focus and timing

A toast never takes focus. The 8-second minimum and the pause on hover or focus meet WCAG 2.2.1, Timing Adjustable. When a focused toast closes, focus returns to the element that was focused before it.

Motion

Toasts slide up and fade in over --duration-base. With reduced motion turned on, they appear without moving. The bell panel appears without animation.

Content

  • Bell: name the person and the thing, in the past tense: "Esther Nakamura cancelled Austin Faith & AI Panel". Link the thing, not the whole sentence.
  • Toast title: what happened, in two or three words: "Invite sent", "You’re going", "Contact restored". The text line says what happens next: "Nate Oyelaran has 14 days to accept."
  • Failures say that nothing was lost: "Your announcement is in the app. Email will send when our provider recovers."
  • Banners say what is true and what to do: "Verify your email to RSVP", then "Send again".
  • Email subjects work without opening the email: "Cancelled: Austin Faith & AI Panel", "You’re going: Toronto Builders Night".
  • Dates use the "Tue 21 Oct" format. Items from today show the time: "Today, 11:42 am".

Do and don't

Sources

  • Bell: unread, info, success, warning, danger, agile, empty; pages/notifications/default
  • Toasts: info, success, warning, danger, stacked
  • Banners: info, success, warning, danger
  • Email: info (invite), success (RSVP), warning (contact signed up), danger (cancelled), announcement, assigned, workspace-added, poker-invite
