# The partner board

## opening: Opening
In this video you move partners along the pipeline on the Kanban board. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the organisations are made up.

## board: Three stages
The board has three columns: Lead, In Funnel and Confirmed. Each column counts its partners. Each card shows the organisation, its main contact and what they offer, and the next step when a lead has logged one.

## drag: Drag a card
To move a partner, drag its card to another column. The card lifts, and a dashed slot shows where it will land.

## drop: Drop it
Let go, and the card joins its new column. The board announces the move for screen readers, and the counts update straight away.

## move-dialog: Move without dragging
You can also use the keyboard. Each card has a Move button that opens a dialog, where you pick the stage the partner moves to.

## note: Add a note
Write a short note for the timeline, such as the day the contract was signed.

## timeline: On the timeline
Confirm, and the card lands in its new column. Open the partner, and a Stage moved entry tops its activity timeline, saying who moved it, from where to where, and the note you left.

## closing: Summary
That is the partner board: three stages, moved by drag or by keyboard, with every move recorded on the partner's activity timeline.
