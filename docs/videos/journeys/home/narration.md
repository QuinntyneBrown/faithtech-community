# Your home page

## opening: Opening
In this video you visit the home page, the first thing you see after you sign in. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## greeting: Greeting
Home greets you by name and tells you today's date and how many events are coming up. Here, a City Lead is signed in on a Wednesday afternoon.

## coming-up: Coming up
Coming up lists the next events in date order, with when and where each one happens, and whether it is public or for members only. All events takes you to the full list.

## quick-actions: Quick actions
Near the top are quick actions. A lead can start a new event or add a contact straight from here, without hunting through the menu.

## pipeline: Partner pipeline
The partner pipeline shows how many partners are at each stage: Lead, In funnel and Confirmed. Open board takes you to the full Kanban board.

## activity: Recent activity
Recent activity gathers what happened lately, such as a contact signing up or an invite being accepted. Each item links to the person it is about.

## closing: Summary
That is the home page: a greeting, the events coming up, quick actions, the partner pipeline and your recent activity, all in one place.
