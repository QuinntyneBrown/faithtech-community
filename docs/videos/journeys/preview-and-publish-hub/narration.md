# Preview and publish a hub

## opening: Opening
In this video a City Lead previews the event hub they filled in, then publishes it. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## home: The hub home
The hub home now has two new buttons: Preview, and Publish.

## preview: Preview
Preview opens the real hub page, the same page visitors will get. A banner says it's a draft, so no one else can open it yet.

## scroll: Only what's ready
Only sections that are shown and ready appear, in the hub's order. Anything still missing is simply left out, so there's never an empty heading.

## wizard: From any wizard
Every section wizard has a Preview link too. It opens the page in a new tab, so the wizard keeps what you've typed.

## publish: Publish
Publish needs nothing else. The hub is live straight away, and the home shows when it was published.

## copy: Share the link
Copy the link to share it with attendees and helpers.

## unpublish: Unpublish
Unpublish asks first. Visitors lose the page until you publish again, but everything it says is kept.

## unpublished: A draft again
Confirm, and the hub is a draft again. Every publish and unpublish goes in the audit log.

## closing: Summary
That's previewing and publishing a hub: the real page one click away, and publishing in one more.
