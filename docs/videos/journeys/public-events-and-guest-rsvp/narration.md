# Public events and guest RSVP

## opening: Opening
In this video you are a visitor with no account, looking at a public event. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## public-page: The share page
Public events have a page anyone can open, with the date, the place and how many people are going. Signing in is optional.

## validation: Name and email
To RSVP as a guest you need a name and a valid email. If something is missing, the form tells you exactly what to fix.

## rsvp: I am going
Fill in both fields and press I'm going. You are on the list, and a confirmation is on its way to your email. No account is created.

## cancel-link: The cancel link
The confirmation email includes a link to cancel. It opens a page that asks whether you really want to.

## cancelled: Cancelled
Confirm, and your RSVP is gone. Using the same link again no longer works.

## closing: Summary
That is public events: a page anyone can share, a quick guest RSVP, and a safe way to cancel.
