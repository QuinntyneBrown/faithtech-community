# Switch method

## opening: Opening
In this video you are a workspace owner changing how your team works, from Scrum to Kanban and back. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## method: The current method
In your workspace settings, the Method row shows that the team uses Scrum, with a button to switch to Kanban.

## warning: What will change
Before anything happens, a dialog explains it. Items keep their status, and switching to Kanban closes the running sprint and sends its unfinished stories back to the backlog.

## kanban: Now on Kanban
Confirm, and the workspace uses Kanban. The board now shows every story in To Do, In Progress and Done, with no sprint, and your story is still in progress.

## back: And back again
Switching back to Scrum works the same way, and the dialog tells you that every story that is not done goes to the backlog, ready for the next sprint.

## closing: Summary
That is switching method: the owner chooses, nothing is lost, and you are told exactly what will change first.
