# Workspaces and membership

## opening: Opening
In this video you are a City Lead who sets up a workspace for your team and manages who belongs to it. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## empty: No workspace yet
Your workspaces page starts empty, with an invitation to create your first workspace.

## create: New workspace
The New workspace dialog asks for a name, a method, and who should join. Only leads and volunteers can be picked. Leave the name empty and you are asked to give one.

## created: Create it
Name it Toronto team, keep Scrum, pick Ruth, and create it. It appears in your list at once, with its method and member count.

## settings: Workspace settings
Open the workspace and go to its Settings. The owner sees every member listed, and is the only person who can change them.

## add-member: Add a member
Add members offers the leads and volunteers who are not in yet. Choose Marcus, confirm, and he joins with his role shown beside his name.

## remove-member: Remove a member
Removing someone asks you to confirm, because they lose access straight away. Confirm, and Ruth is gone from the list.

## closing: Summary
That is workspaces and membership: the owner creates the workspace and chooses its members, and only members can see it.
