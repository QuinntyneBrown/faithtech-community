# Error and system pages

## opening: Opening
In this video you see what the app shows when something goes wrong. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the failures are staged on purpose.

## not-found: Page not found
If you follow a broken link, you get a friendly page-not-found screen inside the app, with a way home. Search stays in the top bar.

## forbidden: No access
If you open something you are not allowed to see, the page says you do not have access, without revealing what the thing was.

## server-error: Server error
When the server fails, the page says it is not your fault, and shows a reference code. If you contact support, that code lets them find exactly what happened.

## offline: Offline
If your connection drops, a banner under the top bar tells you right away that you are offline. If a page cannot load at all, a full offline page offers to try again.

## back-online: Back online
When the connection returns, the banner clears by itself, and Try again opens the page you wanted.

## closing: Summary
That is how the app handles trouble: clear words, a way forward, and nothing hidden from you or exposed to others.
