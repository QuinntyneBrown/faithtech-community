# Download a hub archive

## opening: Opening
In this video a city lead keeps a copy of an event hub after the night is over. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## home: Download archive
On the hub home, the Your hub card has a Download archive button, right after Share. It's there for people who manage event hubs, whether the hub is a draft or published.

## preparing: Preparing
Press it, and the server builds the archive. While it works, the button says Preparing archive, and pressing it again does nothing.

## downloaded: Downloaded
The zip arrives, named after the event. Inside is one folder with an index page, the parking maps, the speakers' photos and the slides. The page opens straight from disk with no connection, in light or dark, and its times are in the lead's own time zone.

## failed: When it fails
If the connection drops, nothing is lost. A message says the archive couldn't be made, and offers Try again.

## again: Try again
Try again asks once more, and the archive downloads.

## closing: Summary
That's downloading a hub archive: one button on the hub home, one zip that opens offline with its images and slides, for hub managers only, and every download is recorded in the audit log.
