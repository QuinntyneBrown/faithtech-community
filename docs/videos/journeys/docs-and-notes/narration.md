# Docs & notes

## opening: Opening
In this video you write a page in Docs and notes, link it to another page and share it. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## list: Your pages
Notes lists every page you may open, most recently edited first. Filter to the pages you own, or to the pages shared with you, and back to all pages.

## new-page: Create a page
Choose New page. A blank, private page opens, and you give it a title.

## shortcuts: Markdown shortcuts
Write in blocks. Type a hash and a space for a heading, or square brackets for a to-do. The page saves as you type.

## slash: The slash menu
Type a slash to open the block menu, filter it by name and press Enter. Here, a quote.

## link: Link another page
Type two square brackets to link another page by its title. Pick it from the list and the link appears in your text.

## backlink: Backlinks
Open the linked page, and under Linked from you see every page that points to it.

## share: Share the page
Back on your page, the banner says only you can see it. Choose Share, pick Shared, add a person and a role, and save. They get a notification and can read it.

## closing: Summary
That is Docs and notes: block pages that save as you type, links and backlinks between them, and sharing you control, private by default.
