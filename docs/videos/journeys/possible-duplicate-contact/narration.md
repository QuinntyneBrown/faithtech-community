# A possible duplicate contact

## opening: Opening
In this video a lead starts adding a contact they already have, and the form warns them. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## new-contact: Add a contact
Grace meets Nadia again at an event and goes to add her. She chooses Add contact and types the name.

## warning: You already have this contact
When she leaves the email field, the form checks her own contacts for the same email, ignoring capitals and spaces. A warning names Nadia, her organisation, when she was added and how many notes she has.

## choices: Open it, or save anyway
The warning offers to open the contact she already has. Saving is still possible: the button now reads Save as a new contact. Only Grace's own contacts are checked, so another lead's private contacts are never revealed.

## open-existing: The existing contact
Opening the existing contact takes Grace to Nadia's page, with both earlier notes, ready to add another.

## closing: Summary
That is the duplicate check: your own contacts only, a clear way to the one you have, and nothing ever blocked.
