# Manage contacts

## opening: Opening
In this video you keep a list of private contacts, the people you meet who are not members yet. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## list: Your list
Contacts shows only the people you added, each as a card with their photo or initials. They are private to you; no other lead can see them.

## search: Search
Search looks across names, organisations and the notes you wrote. Type a phrase and press Enter, and the list narrows to the match.

## add: Add a contact
To add someone, choose Add contact. If the form is saved with a missing name or a badly written email, it lists exactly what to fix.

## fill: Fill in the details
Fill in the name, email, organisation and city, and write a first note. When you save, you land on the contact, marked as private to you.

## edit: Edit
Choose Edit to change a contact later. Here you add a phone number, and the detail updates as soon as you save.

## in-list: Back in the list
Back in your list, the new contact appears as a card with their organisation and city, a Private label and a View profile button.

## closing: Summary
That is managing contacts: private to you, easy to search, and simple to add and edit.
