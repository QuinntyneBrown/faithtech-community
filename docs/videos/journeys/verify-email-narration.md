# Verify email

## opening: Opening
In this video you confirm your email address, so the community knows your account is really yours. The app is real; the server behind it is a local stand-in, and every detail is made up.

## pending: Waiting for the link
Right after you sign up, this page reminds you to open the link we emailed, and shows which address it went to.

## resend: Send another link
If the email never arrived, you press Send a new link. A message confirms that a fresh link is on its way.

## open-link: Open the link
Now you open the link from the email. The app checks it with the server, and you are in. From here you can go on to set up your profile.

## expired-link: An old link
Links do not last forever. If you open one that has expired, or that you already used, the page says so plainly, instead of failing quietly.

## new-link: Ask for a new one
You type your email address and press Send a new link. The page confirms a new link was sent, so you can finish verifying.

## closing: Summary
That is email verification: a reminder of where the link went, a way to resend it, and a clear path forward when a link has expired.
