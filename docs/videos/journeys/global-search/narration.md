# Search everything

## opening: Opening
In this video you search across the whole community from one place. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## intro: The search page
Before you type, the search page explains what can be found: people, events, partners and more.

## results: Grouped results
Search for a word, such as shelter. Results come back in groups, with a count for each: people, events, partners, your private contacts, and work items in your workspaces.

## private: Only what you may see
Your own private contacts appear here, but another lead's contacts never do. Search only returns what you are allowed to open.

## top-bar: The top bar
The search box in the top bar works from any page. Type and press Enter to land on the same results.

## short: Too short
A single character is too short, so you get a hint and no search runs.

## none: No matches
When nothing matches, the page says so and suggests trying other words.

## closing: Summary
That is global search: one box, grouped results, and nothing you are not allowed to see.
