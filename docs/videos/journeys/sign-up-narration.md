# Sign up

## opening: Opening
Welcome to FaithTech Community. In this video you create an account. Anyone can join, and it takes one short form. This recording runs the real app against a local stand-in for the server, with made-up details.

## open-form: The sign-up form
You start on the Join FaithTech page. It asks for three things: your full name, your email address and a password.

## short-password: A password that is too short
Say you type a short password and press Create account. Nothing is sent. The form tells you what to fix, and puts your cursor straight back in the password field.

## fix-password: Choose a passphrase
A short phrase is the easiest strong password to remember. You type one that is at least twelve characters long, and create the account again.

## check-inbox: Check your inbox
Your account is created. The page asks you to check your inbox, and names the address the verification link went to.

## closing: Summary
That is sign up: one form, clear errors before anything is sent, and a verification email to finish. The next video shows how that email link works.
