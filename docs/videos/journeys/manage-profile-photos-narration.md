# Manage profile photos

## opening: Opening
In this video you add, describe, feature and remove photos on your profile. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the pictures are made-up illustrations.

## add: Add photos
On Edit profile, the Photos section holds up to ten photos. You choose Add photos and pick two pictures. They appear straight away, marked Not saved yet.

## describe-missing: Describe this photo
Every photo needs a description, for people using screen readers. If you save without one, nothing is uploaded, and the form points you to the photo to describe.

## describe: Describe and save
You describe each picture in a few words, and save. Both photos upload, and your first photo becomes your featured photo.

## feature: Feature a photo
To change which photo leads your profile, you choose Feature on another one. Only one photo is featured at a time.

## remove: Remove a photo
To remove a saved photo, you choose Remove. A dialog asks you to confirm, so nothing is deleted by accident.

## profile: On your profile
On your profile, your featured photo now sits beside your name, with your photos below.

## closing: Summary
That is managing photos: up to ten, each one described, one featured, and removed only when you confirm.
