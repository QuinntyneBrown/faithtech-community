# Run the day from a phone

## opening: Opening
In this video a city lead runs a builders night from a phone, changing the event hub while people are in the room. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## home: Quick edits
On a phone, the hub home opens with Quick edits: the three changes a lead makes on the day, as full-width buttons. Changes show on the hub at once.

## update: Post an update
Post an update. Dinner has moved, so say so in a line or two. It goes to the top of the hub straight away.

## move: Move a session
Build time is running long. Move Code freeze to 8:30. The two sessions after it can follow by the same 30 minutes, and that's ticked already.

## moved: Moved
Move it. Demos and wrap up follow, and the code freeze time moves with its session.

## swap: Swap a link
The submission form changed. Swap the link, and the Submit button on the hub points at the new one.

## hub: On the hub
Everyone's hub now shows the update first, and the agenda at its new times.

## live: Open hubs catch up
Hubs that are already open catch up by themselves: every thirty seconds, and straight away when someone comes back to the tab. No reload needed.

## closing: Summary
That's running the day from a phone: three quick edits, live at once, and every open hub keeps up.
