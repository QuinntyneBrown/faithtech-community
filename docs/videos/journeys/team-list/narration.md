# The team

## opening: Opening
In this video you look at the team page, where you find FaithTech's leads and volunteers. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## everyone: The whole team
Team lists everyone who holds a lead or volunteer role, each with their photo when they have one, their home city and their roles.

## leads: Leads only
The role buttons narrow the list. Choose Leads and the volunteers drop away, leaving the city leads and the other leads.

## city: By city
The city filter narrows it further. Pick Toronto and you see only the leads who call Toronto home.

## no-match: Nobody here yet
If nobody matches, the page says so plainly, such as no volunteers in Austin yet, instead of showing an empty screen.

## show-everyone: Show everyone
Show everyone clears both filters and brings the whole team back.

## open-profile: Open a profile
Each name opens that person's profile. Leads also get an Invite someone link, to grow the team.

## closing: Summary
That is the team page: every lead and volunteer in one place, filtered by role and city, one click from a profile.
