# Email notifications

## opening: Opening
In this video you see how email keeps you informed when you are away from the site. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## rsvp: RSVP to an event
When you RSVP to an event, the page confirms you are going, straight away.

## rsvp-email: A confirmation email
A confirmation goes to your inbox too, with the calendar file attached. Emails are queued with the action itself, then sent within minutes and retried if the mail service is busy.

## settings: Your email settings
In Settings, the Email me when card shows what you get by email. Event changes, invites, role changes and the like always reach you.

## opt-out: Turn off announcements
Announcements are the one thing you can switch off. Turn off Email me announcements and the choice is saved straight away.

## others: Everything else still arrives
Turning announcements off does not silence anything else. When an event you are going to changes, the notice still arrives, here and in your inbox. Cancellations, confirmations and invites still reach you, and every link in an email is secure.

## closing: Summary
That is email: reliable, with a plain-text copy, and under your control where it counts.
