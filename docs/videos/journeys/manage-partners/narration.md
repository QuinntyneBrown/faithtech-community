# Manage partners

## opening: Opening
In this video you add a partner organisation and edit its details. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the organisations are made up.

## empty: A fresh pipeline
Partners is one shared list for every lead. When it is empty, the page invites you to start your partner pipeline.

## missing-name: Add a partner
Choose Add partner and a dialog opens. If you add it without an organisation name, it tells you the name is needed.

## fill: Fill in the details
Fill in the organisation, its website, the stage it is at, the main contact, what they could offer and which lead owns the relationship.

## on-board: On the board
Add the partner and it appears in the stage you chose, with its contact and offer on the card. The page counts the partners for you.

## partner-page: The partner page
Open the card to see the whole partner: its stage, its owner, its contact details, and an activity timeline that starts with the day it was added.

## edit: Edit
Choose Edit to change anything. A badly written email is caught first. Fix it, hand the partner to another owner, and save, and the page shows the new details.

## closing: Summary
That is managing partners: one list shared by every lead, easy to add to, and simple to keep up to date.
