# Archive and transfer resources

## opening: Opening
In this video a city lead tidies up an old resource, changes her mind, and a system admin hands another resource to a new owner. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and files are made up.

## resource: An old deck
Grace owns this launch deck, so its page offers her Archive.

## archive: Archive
Archive asks first. It explains that the deck disappears for everyone today, and that after thirty days it's deleted for good, file and all.

## archived: Archived
Archive it, and it's gone from the list, with a reminder of when it can still be restored.

## archived-list: The Archived page
The Archived page lists what Grace has archived, and the day each one will be deleted for good.

## restore: Restore
Changed her mind. Restore puts it back in Resources, with the same visibility as before.

## transfer: Transfer
Esther is stepping back, and her retro should stay looked after. A system admin opens it and chooses Transfer. Only members who can add resources are offered, so Priya picks Nadia.

## transferred: A new owner
Transfer, and Nadia owns it now.

## closing: Summary
That's archiving and transferring resources: archive hides it from everyone, restore it within thirty days or it's deleted, and a system admin can hand it to another contributor.
