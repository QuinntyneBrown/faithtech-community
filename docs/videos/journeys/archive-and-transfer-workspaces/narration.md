# Archive and transfer workspaces

## opening: Opening
In this video you archive and restore work in a workspace, and then a System Admin hands a workspace to a new owner. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## archive-item: Archive a story
As the owner, open a story and choose Archive. The dialog says its tasks are archived with it. Confirm, and you are back at the backlog.

## restore-item: Restore it
Archived items wait in the workspace settings. Choose Restore beside the story and it comes back with its task.

## archive-workspace: Archive the workspace
The owner can archive the whole workspace. Members lose access, but everything is kept, and it moves to the archived list on the Workspaces page.

## restore-workspace: Restore the workspace
Choose Restore there and the workspace is back in your list, as it was.

## owner-lost-role: When an owner loses City Lead
Only City Leads can own workspaces. If an owner loses that role, the System Admin sees a notice on the Admin page naming the workspace that needs a new owner.

## reassign: Choose a new owner
The notice links to the workspace settings. Choose Reassign owner, pick another City Lead, and confirm. The previous owner stays on as a member.

## closing: Summary
That is archiving and transfer: nothing is deleted, anything can be restored, and no workspace is left without an owner.
