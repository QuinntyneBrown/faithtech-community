# People and roles

## opening: Opening
In this video you look through the people directory and change someone's roles. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## directory: The directory
People and roles lists every member with their photo, their roles and their home city. Someone who has not been given a role yet shows No role yet.

## protected-roles: Protected roles
Some people hold roles that only a System Admin can change, such as Staff. Those rows say so, and they have no remove button for you.

## search: Search
Type a name or an email address and press Enter to narrow the list. If nobody matches, you see a friendly message, with a way to invite them instead.

## add-role: Add a role
To give someone a role, choose Add role on their row. You can pick any role a City Lead may grant, and the list updates right away, without a reload.

## remove-role: Remove a role
Removing a role works the same way. The app asks you to confirm first, and then the person goes back to having no role.

## closing: Summary
That is the people directory: search the whole community, and add or remove everyday roles, while protected roles stay with System Admin.
