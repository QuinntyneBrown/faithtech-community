# Archive, restore and delete contacts

## opening: Opening
In this video you archive a contact, bring it back, and delete one for good. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## archive: Archive
When you no longer need a contact, choose Archive on their page. The app asks you to confirm, and says nothing is lost.

## gone-from-list: Gone from the list
Once archived, the contact leaves your main list, so the list only shows the people you are working with now.

## archived-list: The archive
The Archived page keeps them, with the date they were archived. Nothing is deleted yet.

## restore: Restore
Choose Restore and the contact is back in your list, with all their notes exactly as you left them.

## delete: Delete for good
Deleting is different, and it is permanent. From the archive, you must type the word DELETE exactly. Until it matches, the button stays off.

## deleted: Deleted
Type it correctly and the contact is removed for good, along with its notes. That cannot be undone.

## closing: Summary
That is the contact life cycle: archive to tidy up, restore to undo it, and delete only when you are sure.
