# Start a hub from a template

## opening: Opening
In this video a City Lead starts an event hub from a template, then starts another by copying a past hub. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## choices: Starting points
Step two now offers the organisation's templates, Hackathon, Meetup and Workshop, beside Blank and Copy a past hub.

## meetup: A template
Choose Meetup. It knows an evening of talks needs no submission links.

## ticked: Already ticked
Step three arrives ticked for Meetup. Untick anything you don't need.

## filled: Defaults filled in
On the hub home, the Hero and Agenda are already ready. The agenda's times are counted from this event's start.

## copy: Copy a past hub
For the next event, choose Copy a past hub, and search your hubs by title.

## pick: Pick one
Pick last month's hackathon.

## copied: Times moved
Every section comes over. Its agenda and code freeze move to the new date, and what it hid stays hidden.

## closing: Summary
That's starting a hub from a template or a past hub: less typing, and nothing to set up twice.
