# Duplicate partners and merge

## opening: Opening
In this video you find a partner that was added twice and merge the two records into one. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the organisations are made up.

## alert: A possible duplicate
Two leads added Kestrel Labs. The partner page warns that it may be the same partner as Kestrel Labs Inc., says who added it and when, and why it matched: the same website.

## compare: Compare and merge
Compare and merge opens a dialog that lists only the fields that differ, side by side. The values of the partner you are on are chosen to start with.

## choose: Choose what to keep
Fields that are the same on both are kept as they are, and the notes are joined. Here Grace keeps the other record's phone number and hands the partner to Esther.

## merged: Merged
Merge partners keeps one record. A message confirms it, the warning is gone, and a Merged entry tops the timeline.

## both-timelines: Nothing is lost
Both timelines are now on one partner. Entries that came from the other record say so, and Details shows the values Grace chose.

## archived: The archived record
The other record is archived, not deleted. Its page says who merged it, when and into which partner, and it can no longer be edited or logged against.

## not-the-same: Not the same
Sometimes a match is wrong. These are two different churches with the same name, so Grace chooses Not the same, and no lead is warned about this pair again.

## restore: Restore, for System Admins
If a merge was a mistake, a System Admin can open the archived record and choose Restore. The dialog explains what comes back.

## restored: Restored
The partner is its own record again at its old stage, its own entries move back, and a Restored entry tops its timeline.

## closing: Summary
That is duplicate detection and merge: possible duplicates flagged, merged without losing anything, wrong matches dismissed, and merges that can be undone.
