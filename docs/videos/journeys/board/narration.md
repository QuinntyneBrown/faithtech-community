# The board

## opening: Opening
In this video you are a team member using the sprint board. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## board: The sprint board
The board shows the stories in the active sprint in three columns: To Do, In Progress and Done. Above them are the sprint goal and how many points are complete, and each card shows its assignee, its points and its task progress.

## keyboard-move: Move without a mouse
Every card has a Move button. Press Enter on it, choose a column, and confirm, so the board works fully from the keyboard.

## drag: Or drag and drop
If you prefer, drag a card to another column. The server is told straight away, and the card stays where you dropped it.

## live: Everyone sees changes live
When a teammate moves a card on their screen, it moves on yours within a couple of seconds, with no refresh.

## closing: Summary
That is the board: the sprint in three columns, cards you can move by dragging or by keyboard, and changes everyone sees live.
