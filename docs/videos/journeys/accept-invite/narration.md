# Accept an invite

## opening: Opening
In this video you follow an invite link from an email and join FaithTech. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## invitation: Your invitation
The link opens a page that says who invited you, which city, and the role you are joining with. Your email address is already filled in, from the invite.

## short-password: A short password
Enter your name and choose a password. If the password is too short, the page lists what to fix and sends nothing yet.

## join: Join
Choose a longer passphrase and join. You land signed in on your own profile, and the role you were invited with is already there.

## used-link: A used link
An invite link works once. If you open it again, the page says the invite has already been used.

## expired-link: An expired link
Invites also expire after fourteen days. An old link says so, and offers a link to create an account instead.

## closing: Summary
That is accepting an invite: open the link, choose a password, and you are in, with your role, while used and expired links explain themselves.
