# In-app notifications

## opening: Opening
In this video you keep up with what happens in the community through notifications. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## bell: The bell
The bell in the top bar shows how many notifications are unread. Its label says the number too, for screen readers.

## panel: The panel
Press the bell to open a panel with your newest notifications first. Unread ones stand out.

## open-notice: Open a notification
Choosing a notification marks it read and takes you straight to what it is about. The count drops by one.

## see-all: See all
See all opens the full Notifications page, with a count of what is still unread.

## unread: Only unread
The Unread filter hides everything you have already seen.

## mark-all: Mark all as read
Mark all as read clears the rest in one press, and the count disappears.

## closing: Summary
That is notifications: a bell that counts, a quick panel, and a full page when you want everything.
