# View profiles

## opening: Opening
In this video you look at member profiles, which only signed-in members can see. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and pictures are made up.

## own-profile: Your own profile
Your name in the top bar takes you to your own profile. A new profile starts with no role and no photos, and offers Edit profile and Add photos.

## other-profile: Another member
Now you open another member's profile. You see their name, their roles, their home city and a short bio, with their featured photo beside their name.

## photos: Their photos
Further down are their photos, each with a description for people using screen readers. There is no Edit profile button here, because this profile is not yours.

## not-found: Not found
If a profile does not exist, or belongs to someone you may not see, such as a lead's private contact, you simply get profile not found.

## closing: Summary
That is viewing profiles: for members only, with names, roles, a bio and photos, and nothing shown that you are not allowed to see.
