# App shell tour

## opening: Opening
This is a tour of the app shell: the frame around every member page. You will see it on a desktop, a tablet and a phone, in light and dark themes, and with a keyboard. The server is a local stand-in, and the account is made up.

## desktop: Desktop
On a wide screen, the top bar shows the main sections: Home, Events, Contacts, Partners and Workspaces. The rest wait behind More until the screen is wide enough for them. The section you are in is highlighted, and the bell and your photo sit on the right.

## tablet: Tablet
On a tablet the same top bar fits, with More opening the menu, and nothing scrolls sideways.

## phone: Phone
On a phone, the sections move to a tab bar along the bottom: Home, Events, Search, Contacts and More.

## drawer: The menu
More opens a drawer with every section. While it is open, the keyboard stays inside it, and Escape closes it and returns you to the More button.

## dark-theme: Dark theme
The app follows your device's theme. When your device switches to dark mode, the app switches too, with the same accessible contrast.

## light-theme: Light theme
Switch back, and the app returns to the light theme.

## skip-link: Skip to content
Everything works from the keyboard. The first press of Tab reveals a Skip to content link, which jumps straight past the top bar.

## keyboard: Keyboard
Every control shows a clear focus ring as you move through it.

## closing: Summary
That is the app shell: one layout that adapts from phone to desktop, light and dark themes, and full keyboard access, built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
