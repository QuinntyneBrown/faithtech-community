# App shell tour

## opening: Opening
This is a tour of the app shell: the frame around every member page. You will see it on a desktop, a tablet and a phone, in light and dark themes, and with a keyboard. The server is a local stand-in, and the account is made up.

## desktop: Desktop
On a wide screen, the top bar shows every main section: Home, Events, Contacts, Partners, Team, Announcements and People. The section you are in is highlighted. The pages behind most sections are still being built.

## tablet: Tablet
On a tablet the same top bar fits, and nothing scrolls sideways.

## phone: Phone
On a phone, the sections move to a tab bar along the bottom, and a Menu button appears at the top.

## drawer: The menu
Menu opens a drawer with every section. While it is open, the keyboard stays inside it, and Escape closes it and returns you to the Menu button.

## dark-theme: Dark theme
The app follows your device's theme. When your device switches to dark mode, the app switches too, with the same accessible contrast.

## light-theme: Light theme
Switch back, and the app returns to the light theme.

## keyboard: Keyboard
Everything works from the keyboard. The first press of Tab reveals a Skip to content link, and every control shows a clear focus ring as you move through it.

## closing: Summary
That is the app shell: one layout that adapts from phone to desktop, light and dark themes, and full keyboard access, built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
