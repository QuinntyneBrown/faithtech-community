# Build a hackathon hub

## opening: Opening
In this video a City Lead builds a hackathon hub. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## sections: Hackathon sections
A hub started from the Hackathon template holds ten more sections: Parking, What to bring, Partners, Tracks, Process, Rules, Demo format, Judging, Worship and Slides. Each one is optional.

## rules: Rules
Open Rules and add them one at a time. The hub numbers them in this order.

## reorder: Reorder
Move a rule up with its arrow, then save.

## demo: Demo format
In Demo format, set the minutes per demo and add the segments. The running total says when they don't fit.

## fit: It fits
Shorten a segment, and the total says it fits. Save.

## parking: Parking
In Parking, add an option, then choose its map from Resources. Only images are offered.

## map: A map
Pick the map and save. The hub shows it, and only while the section is shown.

## page: The hub page
On the published hub, visitors see each section in order, with the map linked full size.

## slides: Slides
Visitors step through the slides with the arrows, or pick a thumbnail.

## closing: Summary
That's a hackathon hub: ten optional sections, files from Resources, and every number checked as you type.
