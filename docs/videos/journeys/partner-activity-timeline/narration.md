# The partner activity timeline

## opening: Opening
In this video you log a call with a partner and see it join the shared activity timeline. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the organisations are made up.

## timeline: One shared timeline
Every partner page has an activity timeline, newest first. Grace can see the meeting and the email another lead logged, and the day the partner was added, so nobody starts from scratch.

## open-dialog: Log activity
Log activity opens a dialog. Call is chosen already, and the date is today. You can switch to an email, a meeting or a note.

## fill: What happened and what is next
Say what happened in a sentence or two and add the time if you know it. A next step with a due date tells the next lead what to do.

## logged: On the timeline
Log the call, and a message confirms it. The call tops the timeline with Grace's name and the next step under it.

## next-step: Last activity and next step
The header now shows the date of the last activity, and Details shows the next step and when it is due.

## filter: Filter by type
The chips above the timeline show one kind of entry at a time, such as emails, or stage moves. Show all activity brings everything back.

## board: On the board
Back on the board, the partner's card carries the next step too, so the pipeline shows what each partner is waiting on.

## closing: Summary
That is the activity timeline: one shared history per partner, every call, email, meeting and note, and a clear next step.
