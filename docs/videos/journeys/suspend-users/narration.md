# Suspend and reinstate users

## opening: Opening
In this video you are a System Admin, and you suspend an account and then bring it back. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## list: The user list
On the admin users page every active account has a Suspend button next to it.

## dialog: The confirmation
Choosing Suspend opens a confirmation. Focus lands straight in the reason box, so you can start typing.

## reason-required: A reason is required
Try to confirm with the box empty and you are asked to give a reason, so that other admins understand what happened.

## suspend: Suspend the account
Write the reason and confirm. The dialog closes and the row now reads Suspended. The reason is recorded in the audit log.

## reinstate: Reinstate
A suspended account shows a Reinstate button instead. One click, and the person is Active again.

## closing: Summary
That is suspending users: a reason every time, a quick way back, and a record of both in the audit log.
