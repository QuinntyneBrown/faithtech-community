# Check in at the door

## opening: Opening
In this video an Event Lead checks people in at the door of tonight's event. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## event-page: Check in
On the event page, leads see a Check in button first. Check-in opens a day before the event starts.

## list: The attendee list
The check-in page lists everyone who RSVP'd, members and guests, in the order they signed up. The meter counts how many are here.

## search: Find and check in
Type a few letters of a name or email and the list narrows as you type. Turn on the switch, and the check-in is saved at once with the time.

## undo: Undo
Checked in the wrong person? The message has an Undo button, and turning the switch off works too.

## check-in-more: As people arrive
As people arrive, turn on their switches one by one. The meter climbs with each one.

## walk-in: Add a walk-in
Someone who didn't RSVP turns up. Add walk-in asks for their name and, if you like, an email. Check them in now is on already.

## walk-in-added: On the list
The walk-in joins the end of the list, checked in. Walk-ins are counted as here but not as RSVPs.

## export: Export the attendance
Export CSV downloads the attendance, one row per person, with when they RSVP'd and when they were checked in.

## attendance: On the event page
Back on the event page, leads and Staff now see how many people were checked in, beside the RSVP count.

## closing: Summary
That is check-in: find someone, one switch, undo when needed, walk-ins at the door, and attendance you can export.
