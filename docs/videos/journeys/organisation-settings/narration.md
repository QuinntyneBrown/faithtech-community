# Organisation settings

## opening: Opening
In this video you are an administrator running your organisation's settings. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## general: The General tab
The Organisation section opens on General: the organisation's name, its short name, and whether it has chapters.

## rename: Rename the organisation
Choose Edit, change the name and short name, and save. A message confirms it, and the new name shows everywhere straight away.

## roles: Roles
The Roles tab lists every role in order, with how many members hold it and what it lets them do.

## stages: Stages and types
Stages and types holds the partner stages, the event types and the resource types, each in the organisation's own order.

## audit: The audit log
View the audit log to see every settings change, with who made it and when. The rename is the newest entry.

## closing: Summary
That is Organisation settings: the name, roles, chapters, stages and types, every change recorded, and open only to those who may change them.
