# A fast, accessible, secure site

## opening: Opening
This video is about the things every screen does without being asked: keeping the preview private, checking what people type, keeping their text harmless, and working by keyboard, in dark mode and on a phone. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## wall: The private preview
While the site is in private preview, a visitor without the passphrase sees only this neutral wall. It names nothing, shows no images, and nothing behind it loads.

## refused: A wrong passphrase
A wrong passphrase is refused the same way every time, and the field is ready to try again. After five refusals, the visitor is locked out for a while, even with the right one.

## unlocked: Unlocked
The right passphrase, entered from the keyboard, opens the page the visitor first asked for: here, sign up.

## validated: Clear errors
Submit an empty form, and a summary at the top says how many things to fix, and each field says what's wrong with it.

## encoded: Text stays text
Even an email address written to look like code is shown exactly as typed, as text. Nothing anyone types ever runs on the page.

## keyboard: Keyboard
Every control can be reached with the Tab key, and the one you're on always has a visible focus ring.

## dark: Dark mode
The theme follows the device. Switch it to dark, and every screen follows, with the same contrast.

## phone: On a phone
And at phone width, the same page fits: nothing scrolls sideways, and every button is big enough to tap.

## closing: Summary
That's what every screen does for you: a wall in front of the private preview, clear errors and text that stays text, and keyboard, dark mode and phones everywhere.
