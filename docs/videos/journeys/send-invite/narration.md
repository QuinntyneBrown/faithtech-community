# Send an invite

## opening: Opening
In this video you invite a new person to FaithTech and see how invites are tracked. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## invites-list: Your invites
The Invites page lists every invite that leads have sent, with the role it comes with and its status: Pending, Accepted or Expired.

## resend: Send again
An invite expires after fourteen days. Choose Resend on an expired one and it goes out again, and returns to Pending.

## open-dialog: Invite someone
To invite someone new, choose Invite someone. A dialog opens with the cursor already in the Email field.

## bad-email: A mistyped address
If the address is not complete, the dialog tells you what to fix before anything is sent.

## fill: Role and note
Enter their email, choose a role they will join with, from the ones you are allowed to grant, and add a short personal note.

## sent: Sent
Choose Send invite. The dialog closes, a confirmation appears, and the new invite shows at the top of the list, waiting for them.

## closing: Summary
That is sending an invite: an email, an optional role and a note, with every invite and its status in one list.
