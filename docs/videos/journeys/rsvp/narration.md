# RSVP to an event

## opening: Opening
In this video you reserve a place at an event, and then change your mind. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and events are made up.

## details: The event page
An event page shows when and where it happens, who is hosting, and how many people are going. As a regular member you see the count, not the names.

## rsvp: RSVP
Press RSVP and you are on the list. The page says you are going, the count goes up by one, and you get a confirmation by email with a calendar file.

## keep: Think again
If your plans change, Cancel my RSVP asks you to confirm first. Keep my RSVP leaves everything as it was.

## cancel: Cancel your RSVP
Confirm, and your place is released. The host sees one fewer person coming, and the page tells you your RSVP is cancelled.

## again: RSVP again
Changed your mind again? RSVP is back, and you are on the list once more.

## closing: Summary
That is RSVPs: one press to join, a confirmation to keep, and an easy way out that asks before it acts.
