# Add and share resources

## opening: Opening
In this video a city lead adds files and links to the community's shared library, and shares one with just the people who need it. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and files are made up.

## list: Resources
Resources is where decks, templates, training and logos live, so nobody has to dig through old emails for them.

## upload: Upload a file
Add resource, and choose a file. The title comes from its name, and the type is guessed from its extension: a PPTX is a deck.

## details: Details
Give it a better title and a tag. Global is the default: every signed-in member can find it.

## added: Added
Add resource, and it's at the top of the list, with its type, format and size.

## link: Add a link
Not everything is a file. A link works too, as long as it starts with https, like this poster template.

## share: Share it
This pitch is only for some people. Choose Shared, then add Hannah, and everyone with the Volunteer role. Each of them gets a notification.

## shared: Shared
Add and share, and it's in Grace's list, marked as shared.

## recipient: The recipient
Samuel is a volunteer. His notification opens the resource, which says who shared it with him, and that he can open and download it but not share it on.

## closing: Summary
That's adding and sharing resources: upload files or add a link, titles and types guessed for you, and private, shared or global, as each one needs.
