# The audit log

## opening: Opening
In this video you are a System Admin reading the audit log, the record of everything admins have done. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## entries: When, who and what
Each entry shows when it happened, who did it, and what they did, such as granting the City Lead role to someone. The newest entries come first.

## export: Export to CSV
Choose Export CSV and the log downloads as a spreadsheet file, and a message confirms it.

## filter: Filter by category
The Action filter narrows the log to one kind of action. If nothing of that kind happened in the last ninety days, you are told so.

## show-all: Show everything again
Choose Show all actions and every entry comes back.

## closing: Summary
That is the audit log: when, who and what for every admin action, with filtering and export, and only for System Admins.
