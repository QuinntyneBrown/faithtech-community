# Forgot and reset password

## opening: Opening
In this video you reset a forgotten password. You will see the real app working against a local stand-in for the server, with made-up details only.

## forgot-link: Forgot your password
On the sign-in page, you choose Forgot your password. The next page asks for the email address on your account.

## bad-email: A mistyped address
If the address is not a real email, the form says so before anything is sent, and keeps your cursor in the field.

## request: Request the link
With a proper address, you press Send reset link. The answer is the same whether or not an account uses that address, so nobody can use this page to find out who is a member.

## open-link: Open the reset link
Next you open the link from the email, and choose a new password.

## mismatch: Two things to fix
If the new password is too short, and the confirmation does not match, each field shows its own error, and the summary counts what to fix.

## save: Save the new password
You type a long passphrase twice and save it. Your password is updated, and any other devices are signed out for your safety.

## expired: An expired link
A reset link only works once and for a short time. Opening an old one shows that it has expired, and offers to send a new link.

## closing: Summary
That is password reset: a private request, clear errors, a safe new password, and a simple way back when a link has expired.
