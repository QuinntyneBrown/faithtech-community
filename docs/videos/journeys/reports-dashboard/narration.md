# Reports

## opening: Opening
In this video a Staff member opens Reports to see how the community is doing across its cities. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people, events and partners are made up.

## nav: Reports in the menu
Staff and System Admins have a Reports item in the menu. Nobody else sees it. Reports opens on all cities and the last 30 days.

## figures: Five figures
Five figures sit at the top: members, events held, RSVPs, partners confirmed and announcements. Each has a line of context, such as how many members are new or what share of RSVPs checked in.

## charts: Two charts
Members by city shows the largest cities and the rest as Other. New members by month shows each month of the period, with this month so far. Every bar carries its number as text.

## tables: Two tables
The partner pipeline is counted by stage for each city. Events and attendance lists each event with how many were going and how many checked in, and every title links to its event.

## filter: Filter and bookmark
Pick a city and a period and choose Apply. The report reloads for them, and the address carries the filters, so the view can be bookmarked or shared.

## export: Export
Export CSV downloads the figures, both charts and both tables for the filters shown. Private contacts are never read or counted.

## closing: Summary
That is Reports: the city's numbers in one place, filtered by city and period, ready to bookmark and export.
