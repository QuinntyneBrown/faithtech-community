# Read announcements and opt out

## opening: Opening
In this video you read the announcements sent to you, and choose how you want to hear about them. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## list: Your announcements
The Announcements page lists what was sent to you, newest first, with the sender and the message. You only see announcements addressed to you.

## settings: Email settings
Settings has a switch named Email me announcements. It is on by default.

## opt-out: Turn it off
Switch it off and it is saved straight away. You still see announcements here, but they are no longer emailed.

## unsubscribe: Unsubscribe from an email
Every announcement email has an unsubscribe link. It works without signing in, and confirms that you are unsubscribed.

## broken: A broken link
A link that is old or made up says it does not work, and points you to Settings instead.

## closing: Summary
That is reading announcements: everything in one list, and an easy way to stop the emails.
