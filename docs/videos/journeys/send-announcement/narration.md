# Send an announcement

## opening: Opening
In this video a Communications Lead sends an announcement to part of the community. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## compose: New announcement
The New announcement page starts with who it is going to. The Send button always tells you how many people will receive it.

## errors: Subject and message
Try to send an empty announcement and the form lists what is missing. Nothing is sent.

## audience: Choose the audience
Send to everyone, to people with a role, or to an event's RSVP list. Pick People with a role, choose Product Engineer, and the count updates.

## write: Write it
Add a subject and a message. Also send by email is on by default, so people who read email get it too.

## sent: Sent
After you send, you see how many people got it, and how many will also get an email. People who opted out of announcement emails still see it in the app.

## closing: Summary
That is announcements: a clear audience, a clear count, and respect for people's email choices.
