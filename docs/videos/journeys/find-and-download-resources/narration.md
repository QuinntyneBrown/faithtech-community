# Find and download resources

## opening: Opening
In this video a volunteer finds what they need in Resources and downloads it. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people and files are made up.

## list: Resources
Samuel sees every resource he may open, newest first, each with its type, format, size and who added it. One was shared with volunteers only, so it's here for him too.

## filter: Filter
Getting ready for the hackathon? Pick the hackathon tag, then the Deck type, and there's the kickoff deck.

## search: Search
Or search. Typing judges finds the judging rubric through its description, not just its title.

## no-results: Nothing matches
When nothing matches, the page says so, and explains that search covers titles, descriptions, tags and file names, not the text inside files. Clear search brings everything back.

## download: Download
Download saves a file under its original name.

## details: The resource page
And each resource has its own page, with its details, who owns it, and Download.

## closing: Summary
That's finding and downloading resources: only what you may open, filters and search to narrow it down, and every file under its original name.
