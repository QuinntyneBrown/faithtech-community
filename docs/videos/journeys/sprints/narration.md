# Sprints

## opening: Opening
In this video you are the owner of a Scrum workspace, and you close one sprint and start the next. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## active: The running sprint
The backlog shows the active sprint at the top, with its goal, how many stories and points it holds, and when it ends.

## history: Sprint history
Switch to the history view for past sprints. Each shows its dates, its goal and the points completed out of those committed, with the average across sprints at the bottom.

## close: Close the sprint
From the board, choose Close sprint. The dialog tells you how many points were completed and lists the unfinished stories, which go back to the backlog and keep their status.

## start-dialog: Start the next one
Close it, and the board waits for a new sprint. Now start one. It is named for you, every story from the backlog comes checked, and it reminds you of your recent average. Try to start without a goal and it is flagged.

## start: Set the goal and pick stories
Write a goal and untick any story that stays behind. Start the sprint, and the board is running again.

## closing: Summary
That is sprints: one at a time, each with a goal and its stories, and unfinished work always comes back to the backlog.
