# Link a contact on sign-up

## opening: Opening
In this video a person you keep as a contact joins FaithTech, and you link the two. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## joined: They joined
When someone with the same email as one of your contacts signs up, your contacts list tells you, and their row is marked Signed up.

## match: A match
Open the contact and you see a notice that they have joined FaithTech, with a choice to link them or say it is not them.

## link-dialog: Link their profile
Choosing Link to their profile asks what to do with your notes. By default, they stay private to you.

## linked: Linked
Confirm, and the contact is now marked as a member with a linked profile. Your private notes are still there, and still only yours.

## new-contact-member: Already a member
The same happens when you add a contact whose email already belongs to a member. The form tells you, and Save and link finishes the job in one step.

## closing: Summary
That is linking a contact: you are told when they join, you choose to link them, and your private notes stay private.
