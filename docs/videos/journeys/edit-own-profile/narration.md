# Edit your profile

## opening: Opening
In this video you edit your own profile, so other members can find and know you. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the details are made up.

## open-edit: Edit profile
From your profile, you choose Edit profile. The form holds your name, your home city and a short bio.

## empty-name: A missing name
If you clear your name and save, nothing is sent. The form asks for the name you would like people to see, and moves your focus to a summary of what to fix.

## fill: New details
You type your name, your home city and a sentence about yourself, then press Save profile.

## saved: Saved
A message confirms that your profile is saved.

## back: Back on your profile
You go back to your profile, and the new details are there for other members to see.

## closing: Summary
That is editing your profile: three simple fields, clear errors before anything is sent, and a confirmation when it is saved.
