# Contact notes

## opening: Opening
In this video you keep notes on a private contact. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## existing: A contact with a note
Open a contact and you see their details and the notes you wrote before, each with its date.

## open-dialog: Add a note
Choose Add note. A dialog opens, with the cursor ready in the note field.

## empty-note: An empty note
If you try to add an empty note, the dialog asks you to write something first, and nothing is saved.

## write: Write the note
Write what you want to remember, such as what the person asked for, and choose Add note.

## newest-first: Newest first
The dialog closes and your note appears at the top of the list, with today's date, above the older one.

## closing: Summary
That is contact notes: quick to add, dated, newest first, and visible only to you and System Admin.
