# Planning Poker

## opening: Opening
In this video you are a workspace member who facilitates a Planning Poker session, so the team can estimate stories together. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## no-session: No session yet
The Planning Poker tab starts with no session running, and a button to start one.

## start: Start a session
Choose the stories to estimate and the members to invite. If you forget the stories, you are reminded. Start and invite, and the first story is up for estimating.

## vote: Vote in private
Pick a card from the deck. As teammates vote, you see who has voted, live, but never what they chose.

## reveal: Reveal the votes
When everyone has voted, the facilitator reveals them all at once. If the votes differ, the page shows the range and suggests the highest and lowest voters explain their thinking.

## revote: Talk, then vote again
After the discussion, start a re-vote. Everyone chooses again, and this time the team agrees on eight.

## set-points: Save the points
Set the final points, and they are saved to the story. The session moves straight on to the next one.

## closing: Summary
That is Planning Poker: private votes, a reveal for everyone together, and points saved to each story.
