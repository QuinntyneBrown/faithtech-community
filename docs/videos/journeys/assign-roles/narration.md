# Assign roles

## opening: Opening
In this video you are a City Lead, and you give people the roles they need. The app is real; the server is a local stand-in, and the people are made up.

## profile: Start on a profile
Open a member's profile. Their roles show as badges, and because you are a City Lead you also see an Add role button.

## add-dialog: The role choices
The dialog offers only the roles you may grant: Lead, Volunteer, Product Engineer and Attendee. City Lead, Staff and System Admin can only be granted by a System Admin, and the dialog says so.

## choose: Add a role
If you confirm without choosing, you are asked to pick one. Choose Volunteer, confirm, and the new badge appears at once, with no page reload.

## remove: Remove a role
Removing works the same way. Open Ruth's profile, choose Remove role, and confirm, and she goes back to having no role yet.

## closing: Summary
That is assigning roles: City Leads manage the everyday roles, and only System Admins grant the powerful ones.
